Daiso Korea is run by the Korean company Asung Daiso. Below: the price system, what is worth buying, the big store, and the tax refund.

## Six prices, nothing above ₩5,000

Every item at Daiso Korea costs one of six fixed prices. Daiso started in 1997 with four prices up to ₩2,000, added ₩3,000 in 2004 and ₩5,000 in 2006, and in 2026 Korean press reported the company still had no plan to raise the ₩5,000 ceiling.

{{fig:daiso-1|Daiso Korea's six fixed prices}}

What this means for you: the price is on the tag and there is no bargaining. To stay under ₩5,000, some products come in smaller sizes than the versions sold elsewhere (see the skincare example below).

## What visitors actually buy

We are not going to give you a "best of" list built on nothing. Here is what Korean press has reported, and what follows from it.

- **Budget skincare from known Korean brands.** Brands make small, low-priced versions for Daiso. The best-known case: VT's Reedle Shot, sold at Daiso as six 2 ml sticks for ₩3,000, sold out across stores in 2023–24. In 2024 sub-brands of Amorepacific and LG H&H also launched lines there, and by 2025 press counted well over 100 beauty brands at Daiso.
- **Snacks and small souvenirs.** Cheap enough to buy for a whole office or class.
- **Trip fixes.** Things you forgot or need for the flight home: pouches, organisers, small bags, toiletries, phone accessories.

Who does not need Daiso: if you only want full-size skincare from one brand, or want to compare many products with testers, Olive Young is the better trip.

## The big store: Myeongdong Station

The Myeongdong Station branch reopened in March 2023 as a 12-floor store (floors 1 to 12, about 1,650 m²). Korean press called it the second-largest Daiso in Seoul, after the Gangnam Express Bus Terminal branch. At reopening there was a floor guide by the lifts in Korean, English, Japanese and Chinese.

{{fig:daiso-2|How to shop a big Daiso in 15 minutes}}

1. Check the floor guide at the lift and pick your two or three floors.
2. Go to the beauty floor first if skincare is on your list; popular items run out.
3. Pick up everything for one receipt before you queue. Separate receipts cannot be added up for a tax refund.
4. At the till, show your passport if you want the tax refund, then pay.

> Hours and floor layouts change. Check the branch on Daiso's official store finder ([daiso.co.kr](https://www.daiso.co.kr)) or Naver Map before you go. Other Myeongdong, Hongdae and Sinchon branches are smaller but also busy with visitors; see [Seoul areas](/seoul-areas/).

## Tax refund at Daiso

Yes, at many tourist-area branches, but not at every store. Korea's tourism listings show branches such as Myeongdong Station, Myeongdong, Sinchon and Seoul Station as tax refund shops.

{{fig:daiso-3|Can I get a tax refund at Daiso?}}

1. Look for a "Tax Free" or "Tax Refund" sign at the branch.
2. Make sure one receipt reaches ₩15,000. With a ₩5,000 ceiling, that is at least three items.
3. Show your physical passport before you pay.

The rules (₩15,000 minimum per receipt, refund limits, fees, what you actually get back) are on our [tax refund page](/tax-refund/). On a ₩15,000–₩50,000 Daiso receipt the refund is small, a few thousand won at most, so do not change your plans for it.

## Paying

Cards are the normal way to pay in Korea. If the card machine offers to charge you in your home currency, choose won; converting at the till usually costs more. Our [money page](/money/) covers cards, cash and WOWPASS.

## What not to expect

- **Not everything is ₩1,000.** Prices go from ₩500 to ₩5,000, so check the tag.
- **Not the full product.** Beauty items are often smaller sizes made for Daiso, not the full-size version sold elsewhere.
- **No guaranteed stock.** Viral items sell out, and small branches carry a narrower range.
- **Not the same store everywhere.** Floor plans, hours and tax refund service differ by branch.
