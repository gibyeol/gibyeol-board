## The one-line judgment

| Situation | Do this | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Your stop is far from a subway station | Take the bus, with a loaded card | Some routes take no cash at all |
| Getting off any bus | Tap your card again by the exit | Without that tap you lose the transfer discount |
| Unsure where to get off | Follow the bus live in Naver Map | You see the stops coming, in your language |
| Out after the last train | An N bus, ₩2,500 | 14 night routes run until about 6 a.m. |

This page is only about Seoul's city buses. Subway, taxis, last trains and which card to buy are on [Getting around Seoul](/getting-around/). Airport to hotel is on [Incheon Airport to Seoul](/airport-to-seoul/). Who does not need this page: if your hotel and sights are all near subway stations, the subway alone is simpler.

## Read the colour first

The colour of a Seoul bus tells you how far it goes, so you can tell at a glance if it is the right kind of bus.

{{fig:seoul-bus-1|Seoul bus types and adult card fares}}

- **Blue (trunk)** buses cross the city on long routes. **Green** buses are short feeders to stations; the smaller village buses charge ₩1,200.
- **Yellow** buses loop around the centre.
- **Red** express buses run out to the suburbs at ₩3,000. The Climate Card short-term pass does **not** cover them.
- **Route numbers starting with N** are night buses.

## How to ride, step by step

Seoul's rule is simple: tap when you get on, tap again when you get off.

1. **Load your card before you go.** T-money or WOWPASS (it has T-money inside). Where to buy and top up: [Essentials](/essentials/).
2. **Check the route number** in Naver Map or Kakao Map, then match it on the front of the bus.
3. **Tap your card on the reader** as you board.
4. **Watch your stop come up** on the map, and get ready a stop early.
5. **Tap again on the reader by the exit door,** then get off.

{{fig:seoul-bus-2|Tap in when you board, tap out when you get off}}

> Seoul says that without the exit tap you can't get a transfer, so your next bus or subway charges a full fare again.

**Cash:** Seoul runs some routes as cashless buses. Seoul's backup for riders without a card is paying later by bank transfer, which most visitors can't use, and Digital Daily reported in September 2026 that visitors on those routes have to buy and load a Korean transport card. We could not confirm a current route list, so plan as if no bus takes cash.

## Transfers: the 30-minute window

A transfer is not a new fare as long as you board within the window and tapped off.

{{fig:seoul-bus-3|How long you have to transfer after tapping off}}

1. **Tap off** when you leave the first bus.
2. **Board the next bus or subway within 30 minutes** (60 minutes between 9 p.m. and 7 a.m.).
3. **You pay one base fare** for the first 10 km, plus ₩100 for every 5 km after that, for up to four transfers.

Re-boarding the same route does not count as a transfer. The full fare rules are on [Getting around Seoul](/getting-around/).

## Where to get off

Our call: let the app do the watching. Naver Map gives walking, subway and bus directions in English, Japanese and Chinese as well as Korean, and Kakao Map also shows bus routes. Open the route before you board, keep the screen on, and move to the exit door when your stop is next.

## Airport buses are a different thing

City buses and airport limousine buses look like they belong together, but they are separate services.

| | City bus | Airport limousine bus |
|---|---|---|
| Fare (adult) | ₩1,200–3,000 | About ₩16,000–18,000 into Seoul |
| Ticket | Tap your card | Counter or kiosk in arrivals; cards and T-money accepted |
| Big suitcases | Not what it is for | Travel under the bus |

Watch out for night numbers: the late-night airport buses, such as **N6701** and **N6002**, also start with N, but cost about ₩17,000–18,000. They are not the ₩2,500 city N buses. Details on [Incheon Airport to Seoul](/airport-to-seoul/).

## Night buses

After the subway stops, Seoul runs **14 night bus routes** with numbers starting with N, from about 11:10 p.m. to 6 a.m., at ₩2,500 for adults. Look the route and the next bus up in Naver Map before you leave the bar, not at the stop.

## Common mistakes

1. **Getting off without tapping.** The next ride costs a full fare.
2. **Boarding with no card or an empty one.** Some routes take no cash.
3. **Taking a red bus on a Climate Card pass.** It isn't covered.
4. **Waiting too long between rides.** After 30 minutes (60 at night) the transfer is gone.
5. **Mixing up airport N buses and city N buses.** The fares are about seven times apart.
