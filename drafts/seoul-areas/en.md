| If you are | Stay in | Because |
|---|---|---|
| On your first trip and want everything easy | Myeongdong | Airport bus 6001 stops there, and it is Seoul's main shopping street for visitors |
| On a short trip, catching a KTX or an early flight | Seoul Station | The AREX express runs nonstop to the airport, and you can check in for some flights at the station |
| Out late, or here for the street life | Hongdae | The AREX all-stop train stops at Hongik Univ., about an hour from the airport |
| Here for palaces, old Seoul and bars in old alleys | Jongno, Insadong or Euljiro | It is walking distance from the palaces, and buses 6002, 6011 and 6701 serve the area |
| Shopping for clothes, happy to stay up late | Dongdaemun | Buses 6001 and 6702 serve it, and its shopping runs late into the night |

| Going to a concert at KSPO Dome, or here for COEX | Gangnam | It is on the right side of the river, and bus 6020 goes there |

Our pick for most readers is the first three. Each has a one-seat ride from Incheon, and that matters more on arrival day than any neighborhood vibe.

## The three we'd pick first

### Myeongdong: the default for a first trip

Myeongdong is where visitors shop. Foreign cards spent 27.8% of their national total there in May 2026 (Korea Tourism Organization data, reported by the Korea Herald), and more than 20 large fashion and beauty stores opened in the area in two years. Olive Young is among the brands that opened big stores aimed at visitors here (see our [Olive Young](/olive-young/) and [tax refund](/tax-refund/) guides).

- **From the airport:** limousine bus 6001, about 1 hr 20 min, or AREX to Seoul Station then Line 4, about 1 hr 10 min (Visit Seoul, June 2026).
- **Honest downside:** it is built around visitors. The main streets are crowded until the shops close, and you will see more tourists than locals.

### Seoul Station: best for short stays and early flights

Seoul Station is a rail hub, not a neighborhood to wander. It has the KTX, Lines 1 and 4, and the AREX express, which runs nonstop to the airport in about 43 minutes for ₩13,000.

The big reason to stay here is the **city airport terminal**. If you ride the AREX express, you can check in, drop your bags and clear passport control at the station before you go. Check-in runs 05:20–19:00 and closes 3 hours before departure for Terminal 1 (3 hr 20 min for Terminal 2). It works only for flights leaving Incheon the same day, and only some airlines take part, so check yours before you plan around it.

- **Honest downside:** there are few streets for an evening walk, and the station is big enough that the wrong exit costs time. Check the exit number in Naver Map before you set off.

### Hongdae: the young, late-night choice

Hongdae is the area around Hongik University, the city's best-known late-night district. How busy does it get? On Halloween 2026 the city estimated up to 125,000 people there in one night, about four times the crowd in Itaewon.

- **From the airport:** the AREX all-stop train goes straight to Hongik Univ. in about an hour for ₩4,750 with a transport card (Visit Seoul, June 2026). Bus 6002 takes about 1 hr 10 min.
- **Honest downside:** weekend nights are loud. If you are a light sleeper, choose a hotel a few blocks from the main streets.

## The other four, and who they suit

### Jongno, Insadong and Euljiro: for old Seoul

Stay here for the palaces, Insadong, and Euljiro's bars hidden in old printing alleys. Locals call that last area "Hipjiro". Buses 6002 (Jongno), 6011 (Gyeongbokgung and Changdeokgung) and 6701 (Gwanghwamun, City Hall, Euljiro) come from the airport.

- **Honest downside:** no AREX station, so you take a bus or change trains. Bukchon Hanok Village's red zones are open to visitors only 10:00–17:00, so don't expect evening walks there. Euljiro is changing fast as rents rise and print shops move out.

### Dongdaemun: for clothes shopping and night owls

The Korea Times calls Dongdaemun the city's "24-hour fashion center", with Dongdaemun Design Plaza at its heart. Buses 6001 and 6702 come from the airport. If you land late, the night bus N6701 stops at Seoul Station, Euljiro and Dongdaemun Design Plaza, and N6002 stops at Hongdae and Dongdaemun.

- **Honest downside:** the area is at its liveliest when you want to sleep, and there is little to do in the morning.

### Seongsu: for a second trip

Seongsu is the old shoe-making district, now full of pop-up stores and cafes, on Line 2. Seongdong-gu, which includes Seongsu, saw foreign visitors rise 60.2% year on year, against 25.5% for Jung-gu, where Myeongdong is.

- **From the airport:** limousine bus 6013 stops in Seongsu-dong (Airport Limousine route list). By train, take the AREX to Hongik Univ. and change to Line 2.
- **Honest downside:** no rail link to the airport, popular pop-ups have queues, and it is a trip from the palaces. It's better for a second visit than a first.

### Gangnam: for concerts, COEX and business

KSPO Dome, where many K-pop concerts take place, is in Songpa District on the Gangnam side of the river. If your concert is there, stay in Gangnam or Jamsil. If it is at Gocheok Sky Dome (Guro District), Gangnam is the wrong side of the city.

- **From the airport:** bus 6020, about 1 hr 20 min. By train it is about 1 hr 50 min with a change at Hongik Univ. (Visit Seoul, June 2026). Take the bus.
- **Honest downside:** it is the farthest of these from the palaces and old Seoul, and the longest airport trip by rail.

## What we left out, and who doesn't need this

We left out Itaewon, Yeouido, Jamsil and Sinchon. They're fine, but the questions we see are about the seven above. We also don't compare hotel prices, because they change by date and we can't check them fairly.

If your hotel is already booked, skip this page and go to [Incheon Airport to Seoul](/airport-to-seoul/) to pick your ride. Before you land, read up on [money](/money/).
