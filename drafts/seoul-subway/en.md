## The one-line judgment

| Situation | Do this | Why |
|---|---|---|
| A normal 3–7 day Seoul trip | T-money, or WOWPASS (T-money inside) | ₩100 cheaper per ride than a single ticket, and it gets the bus transfer discount |
| You'll ride 4+ separate times a day, only inside Seoul | Climate Card short-term pass | Unlimited Seoul subway and buses from ₩5,000 a day (card extra) |
| You just landed with no card and no cash | Single-journey ticket at a new machine | Since March 2026 some machines take foreign Visa and Mastercard |
| It's past 11:30 p.m. | Check your last train now | The 1 a.m. late service is weekdays only and measured at the end of the line |

This page is only the subway. For buses, taxis and night buses, see [Getting around Seoul](/getting-around/). For the airport trip, see [Incheon Airport to Seoul](/airport-to-seoul/).

## Pick how you pay

Our call: a stored-value card wins for almost everyone; the single ticket is a fallback, not a plan.

{{fig:seoul-subway-1|Which way to pay: start at the top}}

- **T-money** is the standard tap card. **WOWPASS** is a visitor card that has T-money built in, but its transit balance is topped up separately from its money balance (WOWPASS official guide).
- **Climate Card short-term pass** (1, 2, 3, 5 or 7 days: ₩5,000 / ₩8,000 / ₩10,000 / ₩15,000 / ₩20,000, card sold separately) covers Seoul subway and buses. It does **not** cover the Shinbundang Line, GTX or red express buses, and if you get off outside Seoul it doesn't work — Seoul's own example is boarding at Seoul Station and getting off at Indeogwon.
- **Single-journey ticket:** fare plus ₩100, plus a ₩500 deposit. It works only on the Seoul-area rail network, so it can't carry a transfer onto a bus.

Who does NOT need the Climate Card: anyone doing one or two rides a day, or heading out of Seoul (Suwon, the Shinbundang Line to Pangyo) on the same day.

## Topping up: bring some cash

Our call: keep ₩10,000–20,000 in notes for the card, even if you pay everything else by card.

1. Find the top-up machine near the gates and put your card on the reader.
2. Choose the amount and pay in **cash**. Seoul Metro said in September 2025 that topping up a prepaid card at its new machines is still cash only.
3. Check the new balance on screen before you walk away.

> New machines at 273 stations on Lines 1–8 take foreign Visa and Mastercard (about 3.7% fee), but only for Climate Card short-term passes and single tickets, not for topping up T-money.

Phone options (MobileTmoney on iPhone, card-funded top-ups) are in [Essentials](/essentials/) and [Money](/money/).

## Ride: tap in, follow the number, tap out

Our call: read the line **number** and the direction (the next station or the end of the line), not just the colour.

{{fig:seoul-subway-2|Tap in, change lines inside, tap out}}

1. **Tap in** at the gate. The reader shows your balance.
2. **Find your line by number and colour.** Then check the direction (which end of the line you're heading toward) before you go down to the platform.
3. **Change lines inside the station.** No extra gate; follow the signs for the line number you want.
4. **Tap out** at the exit gate. The fare is set by distance, so the exit tap is not optional.
5. **Single ticket?** Put it in the "Deposit Refund" machine after you exit to get your ₩500 back.
6. **Going on by bus?** Board within 30 minutes of tapping out (60 minutes from 9 p.m. to 7 a.m.) with the same card for the transfer discount.

Each person needs their own card: Seoul's transfer rule is one card per person, so two people can't share one card and still get transfers.

**Exits are numbered.** Directions in Korea are written as "Line 4, Myeongdong Station, Exit 8". Check the exit number in Naver Map or Kakao Map before you go up — in big stations the wrong exit can leave you a long walk from where you meant to be.

**Line 9 has express trains.** Express and local trains use the same platform, at the same fare. If the express doesn't stop at your station, ride it to the nearest express stop and change to a local.

## Fares in one card

Our call: don't try to calculate fares; just know why the card is cheaper.

{{fig:seoul-subway-3|Card vs single ticket, adult fares}}

- Adult base fare: **₩1,550** with a card, **₩1,650** with a single ticket, for the first 10 km (since 28 June 2025). Longer trips add ₩100 per 5 km; full table in [Getting around](/getting-around/).
- Transfers: up to 4 (5 rides) on one card, using the 30-minute window above. Re-boarding the same line doesn't count.

## Rush hour and seats

Our call: if you have bags, avoid roughly 7–9 a.m. on weekdays.

- Seoul's transit-card data puts the morning peak at 7–9 a.m., busiest around 8:10. The most boardings in a day come between 6 and 7 p.m. (2024 figures). Seoul's 2025 data showed Line 9's morning express as the most crowded.
- **Priority seats** at each end of the car are for older people, disabled passengers and others who need them. It isn't illegal to sit there, but locals leave them free.
- **Pink seats** are for pregnant women. Leave them empty even when the car is full; early pregnancy doesn't show, and some women carry a pink badge.

## Last trains

Our call: decide how you get home before dinner, not at 12:40 a.m.

- Since 2022 Seoul runs the late service so that weekday last trains reach the **end of the line around 1 a.m.** Saturdays, Sundays and holidays are not part of that extension, so weekend last trains run earlier.
- The last train from your station leaves earlier than that. Check it in Naver Map or Kakao Map.
- Sections run by Korail (parts of Lines 1, 3 and 4) can differ.

Missed it? Night buses and taxi apps are in [Getting around](/getting-around/).

## AREX: airport train vs the subway

Our call: the **all-stop** AREX is just another subway line for your card; the **Express** is a separate ticket.

- **All-stop train:** stops at every station (including Hongik Univ. and Gongdeok), no seat reservation, tap your card, and it's part of the Seoul-area transfer discount.
- **Express train:** only Seoul Station, Incheon Airport Terminal 1 and Terminal 2, reserved seats, its own fare (₩13,000 adult on the AREX booking page).

Full comparison: [Incheon Airport to Seoul](/airport-to-seoul/).

## Common first-day mistakes

1. **Skipping the exit tap.** You lose the transfer and can be charged extra.
2. **Throwing away the single ticket.** That's your ₩500 deposit.
3. **Trying to top up T-money with a credit card.** Station top-ups are cash.
4. **Using the Climate Card on the Shinbundang Line or past Seoul's edge.** It won't cover it.
5. **Boarding a Line 9 express that skips your stop.** Check the stop list on the platform.
6. **Assuming trains run past midnight on weekends.** They don't run as late as weekdays.
