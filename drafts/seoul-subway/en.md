## The one-line judgment

| Situation | Do this | Why |
|---|---|---|
| A normal 3–7 day trip | T-money, or WOWPASS (T-money inside) | ₩100 cheaper per ride than a single ticket, plus bus transfers |
| 3+ rides a day for 3+ days, all in Seoul | Climate Card short-term pass | 3 days: ₩10,000 + ₩3,000 card, about eight fares |
| No card, no cash | Climate Card or single ticket at a new machine | Foreign Visa/Mastercard accepted since 17 March 2026 |
| It's late evening | Check your last train now | Weekends stop earlier, and times may have moved |

Subway only here. Buses and taxis: [Getting around Seoul](/getting-around/). Airport: [Incheon Airport to Seoul](/airport-to-seoul/).

## Pick how you pay

Our call: a stored-value card suits almost everyone.

{{fig:seoul-subway-1|Which way to pay: start at the top}}

- **T-money** is the standard tap card. **WOWPASS** has T-money built in, but its T-money balance is topped up separately (WOWPASS guide).
- **Climate Card short-term pass:** 1, 2, 3, 5 or 7 days for ₩5,000 / 8,000 / 10,000 / 15,000 / 20,000, plus ₩3,000 for the card. It covers Seoul's subway and buses until the last train of the final day (a 1-day pass ends that night).
- Not covered: the Shinbundang Line, GTX, express (gwangyeok) and airport buses, and stations outside Seoul. Get off outside Seoul (Seoul's example: Jongno 3-ga to Indeogwon) and staff charge the fare. Exception: you may get off at Incheon Airport T1/T2.
- **Single ticket:** fare plus ₩100, plus a ₩500 deposit. Rail only, so no bus.

Who does NOT need the Climate Card: anyone riding once or twice a day, or leaving Seoul (Suwon, Pangyo) that day.

## Topping up: bring some cash

Our call: keep ₩10,000–20,000 in notes for T-money.

1. Put your card on the top-up machine near the gates.
2. Choose the amount and pay in **cash**. New kiosks take cards for tickets and passes, but T-money top-ups are cash only.
3. Check the new balance before you leave.

> The 440 new machines (273 stations, Lines 1–8) take foreign cards only for the Climate Card and single tickets, with a fee of about 3.7%. WOWPASS holders can move WOWPASS balance to T-money in the app.

## Ride: tap in, follow the number, tap out

Our call: read the line **number** and its end station, not just the colour.

{{fig:seoul-subway-2|Tap in, change lines inside, tap out}}

1. **Tap in** at the gate. The reader shows your balance.
2. **Find your line by number** and check the direction.
3. **Change lines inside.** No extra gate.
4. **Tap out.** The fare depends on distance; a missed exit tap breaks your transfer and costs extra.
5. **Single ticket?** Put it in the deposit refund machine: ₩500 back.
6. **Bus next?** Board within 30 minutes (60 minutes, 9 p.m.–7 a.m.) with the same card.

One card per person: sharing a card loses the transfer discount.

**Exits are numbered** ("Myeongdong Station, Exit 8"). Check yours in Naver Map or Kakao Map.

**Line 9 has express trains** that stop only at marked stations. If one skips your stop, switch to a local at the express stop before it.

## Fares in one card

Our call: let the map app do the maths.

{{fig:seoul-subway-3|Card vs single ticket, adult fares}}

- Adult base fare (10 km): **₩1,550** by card, **₩1,650** by single ticket, since 28 June 2025. Then ₩100 per 5 km to 50 km, ₩100 per 8 km beyond.
- Up to 4 transfers (5 rides) per card. Re-boarding the same route doesn't count.

## Rush hour and seats

Our call: with suitcases, avoid roughly 7–9 a.m. on weekdays.

- Seoul's 2014 transit-card data: 7–9 a.m. carried 20.2% of daily riders; 8:10–8:19 was the busiest ten minutes.
- Line 9's morning express peaked at 192.5% of capacity before Seoul added express trains on 16 November 2026. Metro9 asks commuters to take the local.
- **Priority seats** at each end of the car are for older, disabled and injured passengers and pregnant women. Our advice: don't sit there.
- **Pink seats** (two per car) are for pregnant women; leave them empty. Early pregnancy doesn't show, so some women wear a pink badge.

## Last trains

Our call: decide how you get home before dinner.

- In 2022 Seoul set weekday last trains to reach the end of the line around 1 a.m.; weekends and holidays were excluded.
- In May 2025 Seoul announced moving Lines 1–8 first and last trains 30 minutes earlier from as early as August 2025. We couldn't confirm whether it happened.
- So check your station's last train in Naver Map or Kakao Map. Korail sections of Lines 1, 3 and 4 differ.

Missed it? [Getting around](/getting-around/).

## AREX: airport train vs the subway

Our call: the **all-stop** AREX is just another subway line; the **Express** is a separate ticket.

- **All-stop:** every station (Hongik Univ., Gongdeok…), tap your card, transfers apply.
- **Express:** non-stop Seoul Station–Incheon Airport, reserved seats, ₩13,000 adult.

More: [Incheon Airport to Seoul](/airport-to-seoul/).

## Common first-day mistakes

1. **Skipping the exit tap.** You lose the transfer and pay extra.
2. **Throwing away the single ticket.** That's ₩500.
3. **Topping up T-money by credit card.** It's cash.
4. **Taking the Climate Card out of Seoul.** You pay at the exit.
5. **Boarding a Line 9 express that skips your stop.**
6. **Assuming a fixed last-train time.**
