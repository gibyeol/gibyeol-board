## The short answer

| If you want | Go to | Why |
|---|---|---|
| K-beauty and everything in one walk | Myeongdong | Seoul's main shopping street for visitors, with many cosmetics stores |
| New Korean brands and pop-up stores | Seongsu | Big brand stores and rotating pop-ups along Yeonmujang-gil |
| Young fashion, K-pop goods, a night out | Hongdae | Fashion streets a short walk from the station, busy late |
| Clothes after dark | Dongdaemun | The "24-hour fashion center"; Doota Mall is open until midnight |

This page is about where to **shop**. If you are choosing where to **sleep**, see [where to stay in Seoul](/seoul-areas/).

## The handy fact: they are all on Line 2

Our call: plan your shopping around Line 2. It is a loop line, and it stops at all four areas.

{{fig:shopping-streets-2|Schematic only: the four areas north of the Han River (not to scale)}}

| Area | Station | Exits |
|---|---|---|
| Myeongdong | Myeongdong (Line 4), or Euljiro 1-ga (Line 2) | Exits 5–10 (Line 4), Exit 5 (Line 2) |
| Seongsu | Seongsu (Line 2) | Exits 3 and 4, toward Yeonmujang-gil |
| Hongdae | Hongik Univ. (Line 2, also AREX) | Exit 9 |
| Dongdaemun | Dongdaemun History & Culture Park (Lines 2, 4, 5) | Exit 1 for DDP |

> Check the exit in Naver Map before you go up. In big stations the wrong exit can add a long walk.

## Myeongdong: for beauty and a first trip

Myeongdong is where visitors shop. Foreign cards spent 27.8% of their national total there in May 2026 (Korea Tourism Organization data, reported by the Korea Herald). Visit Seoul describes a mix of street stalls, fashion from high-end to casual, and many cosmetics stores.

- **Best for:** K-beauty, mid-range fashion, street food while you walk.
- **Best time:** our call is earlier in the day. The main streets stay crowded until the shops close.
- **Tax-free:** big chains generally take immediate refunds at the till; Olive Young's Myeongdong flagship has a tax-free counter. Rules are in our [tax refund](/tax-refund/) and [Olive Young](/olive-young/) guides.
- **Skip if:** you want streets where locals shop. Here you will see more tourists than locals.

## Seongsu: for new brands and pop-ups

Seongsu is the old shoe-making district, now full of brand stores, pop-ups and cafes. Most of the main shops sit along Yeonmujang-gil, a few blocks south of Seongsu Station. Musinsa opened its Megastore Seongsu in April 2026, with about 1,000 brands over five floors and a beauty floor on the second level (Korea Herald). Olive Young N Seongsu opened in late November 2024 (Visit Seoul).

- **Best for:** Korean fashion labels, beauty from newer brands, pop-up stores.
- **Best time:** a weekday. The Korea Herald warns that weekends get crowded and aisles are tight.
- **Tax-free:** varies by store. Some handle refunds onsite; at Musinsa and Olive Young, refunds can depend on a minimum single payment or on the branch (Korea Times, June 2025). Ask before you pay.
- **Skip if:** you hate queues, or you are chasing one pop-up you saw online. Pop-ups change every few weeks, so check it is still running.

## Hongdae: for young fashion and K-pop goods

Hongdae is the area around Hongik University. Seoul city data put it at about 40,000 foreign visitors a day, fifth in the city (Korea Times, July 2026). Visit Seoul lists fashion streets and brand flagships within a few hundred meters of Exit 9, and K-Pop Square, the former Line Friends flagship.

- **Best for:** casual and street fashion, K-pop goods, low-priced cosmetics.
- **Best time:** late afternoon into the evening, when the street life starts. It is Seoul's best-known late-night district.
- **Tax-free:** at bigger brand stores; for example, Visit Seoul notes the Covernat flagship offers tax refunds. Smaller shops vary.
- **Skip if:** you want a calm browse. Weekend nights are loud and packed.

## Dongdaemun: for clothes at night

The Korea Times calls Dongdaemun the city's "24-hour fashion center", with Dongdaemun Design Plaza (DDP) at its heart. Doota Mall, about 200 m from DDP, lists hours of 10:30 to midnight every day (Visit Seoul).

- **Best for:** clothes. Doota sits in the Dongdaemun Fashion Town special tourism zone.
- **Best time:** evening to midnight.
- **Tax-free:** we could not confirm it building by building. Bring your passport and ask at the counter.
- **Skip if:** it is morning. There is little to do then, and hours differ by building, so check the one you want.

## A half-day on Line 2

Our call for a first trip with one free afternoon:

{{fig:shopping-streets-3|A half-day shopping route on Line 2}}

1. **Early afternoon, Seongsu.** Exit 3 or 4, walk to Yeonmujang-gil. Weekdays are calmer.
2. **Ride Line 2 west** to Dongdaemun History & Culture Park.
3. **Evening, Dongdaemun.** DDP from Exit 1, then the fashion malls, some open until midnight.
4. **Keep your receipts and passport together.** Over ₩1,000,000 on one receipt, the refund moves to the airport. See [tax refunds](/tax-refund/).

On a beauty-first trip, swap Seongsu for Myeongdong: Euljiro 1-ga is also on Line 2.

## What we left out, and who doesn't need this

We left out Garosu-gil in Sinsa, Hannam-dong and the Gangnam department stores. The sources we could check on them are older or not about shopping by category, so we will add them once we can. We don't rank individual stores or prices, because they change.

If you only want cosmetics, go straight to our [Olive Young guide](/olive-young/). If you are buying big-ticket items, read [duty-free](/duty-free/) first.
