## Our call

| Situation | What to do | Why |
|---|---|---|
| You know what you want (perfume, imported cosmetics, liquor) | Order on a duty-free website or app by the day before, pick up at the airport | Same goods, no store visit, and you collect after passport control on departure day |
| You want to test or compare in person | Visit a downtown duty-free store with your passport and flight details | You pay in the city; the goods wait for you at the airport |
| Last-minute or small buys | Shop at the airport after security | No pickup to plan. Lotte and Hyundai took over Incheon's main cosmetics and liquor zones in 2026 |

What we left out: brand-by-brand price comparisons and membership tier hacks, because prices change daily. Who does not need this page: if your shopping is mostly Korean drugstore beauty, [Olive Young](/olive-young/) with an instant [tax refund](/tax-refund/) is simpler.

## Duty-free vs tax-free: not the same thing

- **Duty-free (면세점):** bonded stores that sell to people leaving Korea. You never take the goods home through the city; you collect them after passport control on the day you leave.
- **Tax-free / tax refund:** normal shops (Olive Young, department stores) that give back part of the 10% VAT, often at the till. That is a separate system, explained on our [tax refund page](/tax-refund/).

You use one or the other for a given purchase, not both.

## How downtown and online pickup works

Whether you buy at a downtown store or online, the goods are handed over only at a **departure pickup counter (인도장)**, inside the restricted area after passport control. Operators say you cannot collect on arrival in Korea.

What you need:

- **Passport.** The number on your order must match the passport you travel with. A photo or copy does not count when buying in store.
- **Flight details.** Departure date, airport and flight number go into the order. In store, bring your e-ticket (paper or on your phone).
- **Boarding pass and exchange voucher** at pickup. Shilla asks online buyers to bring the voucher.

### Order deadlines

Each operator sets its own cutoff by airport and departure time. Hyundai Duty Free's published example for Incheon:

| Pickup at | Your flight leaves | Order by |
|---|---|---|
| Incheon T1 | after 17:00 same day | 11:30 same day |
| Incheon T1 | after 03:00 next day | 19:30 the day before |
| Incheon T2 | after 18:00 same day | 11:30 same day |
| Incheon T2 | after 03:00 next day | 19:30 the day before |

The site shows your real deadline once you enter your flight. Our rule of thumb: **finish ordering the evening before you fly.** Hyundai lets you order up to 90 days ahead.

### Where to pick up at Incheon

All counters are on the 4th floor, after security and passport control:

- **Terminal 1:** East counter (across from gates 10–11) and West counter (across from gates 43–45), plus a counter in the Concourse near gate 119.
- **Terminal 2:** next to gate 252, across a bridge.

Your voucher tells you which counter holds your order. Shilla asks you to collect at least 1–2 hours before departure, so the usual three-hour arrival ([departure day guide](/departure-day/)) leaves time. Check the item names and quantity before you sign. Goods you fail to collect are returned and held for 10 days, then the sale is cancelled and refunded.

## Is there a purchase limit for foreigners?

No Korean limit on what you buy when leaving. Hyundai Duty Free states that customers departing Korea can buy without limit. The $800 figure you see on Korean sites is Korea's allowance for goods brought **into** Korea, which matters to Korean residents, not to you.

The real limit is at home.

## What you can bring into the US (the number that matters)

From the US Customs and Border Protection (CBP) pages:

| | Duty-free allowance |
|---|---|
| Goods | **$800** per person, if you were abroad at least 48 hours and have not used it in the last 31 days |
| Alcohol | **1 liter**, age 21 or over, personal use |
| Tobacco | **200 cigarettes and 100 cigars**, age 21 or over |

CBP is explicit: goods bought in a duty-free shop are **not** automatically duty-free when you return. They count toward your $800 like anything else. State alcohol laws can be stricter.

## Alcohol and liquids

- Liquor bought downtown or online is handed to you after security, so liquid limits do not stop you boarding in Korea.
- Liquids bought in duty-free come in a sealed bag. Keep it sealed until your final destination, especially if you change planes. Our [departure day guide](/departure-day/) has the rules.
- Over 1 liter? You can still bring it, but declare it and pay duty and tax.

## Who runs what in 2026

- **Incheon Airport:** Shilla and Shinsegae handed back the main cosmetics, perfume, liquor and tobacco zones after losses. Lotte (DF1) and Hyundai (DF2) took them over in 2026 on contracts to June 2033. Shilla and Shinsegae still run fashion and boutique zones.
- **Downtown:** all four still trade in Seoul. Press reported in June 2026 that Hyundai is scaling back its Dongdaemun and Trade Center stores while Shilla and Shinsegae focus on downtown and online. Check opening hours before you go.
- **New in July 2026:** foreign visitors can collect Korean beauty and food items bought from online duty-free at downtown duty-free stores, not just at the airport.
