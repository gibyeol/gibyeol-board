## Affiliate links

Some links on Nadri are affiliate links. If you buy or book through one, the shop or booking site pays Nadri a commission. You pay the same price. Pages with affiliate links say so at the top, and affiliate buttons are labelled.

The affiliate links on the site today are Klook and Yesim links through the travel affiliate network Travelpayouts, plus one Stylevana link through Awin on the [K-beauty delivered to your door](/buy-from-home/) page. On pages that carry the affiliate notice, and only if you choose "Accept" in the cookie notice, scripts from Travelpayouts and Skimlinks may turn some travel and shop links into affiliate links ([Privacy](/privacy/)). Applied and waiting for review: Agoda and Trip.com through Travelpayouts, YesStyle through Awin, and Olive Young through Rakuten Advertising. We add them here once approved. Approved but not linked yet: KKday through Travelpayouts (as of Nov 27, 2026).

## What commissions do not change

- We do not accept payment to include, rank or praise a product or place.
- A guide recommends what fits the reader's situation, including free options and options we earn nothing from.
- If a partner's offer is worse than an alternative, we say so.

## Advertising

Nadri may show advertising in the future. Ads will be clearly separated from guide content.
