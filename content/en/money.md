## The short answer

| Where | Use | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Department stores, restaurants, convenience stores, taxis | Your foreign card | Visa, Mastercard, Amex, JCB and UnionPay are widely accepted |
| Gwangjang or Namdaemun market food, street stalls | Cash | Many small stalls have no terminal for foreign cards |
| Subway and bus | A transport card | You can't tap a foreign bank card at the gate yet |
| Delivery and booking apps | Depends on the app | See "When your card is rejected" below |

## How much cash

Our call: about ₩100,000 covers a 3–5 day Seoul trip. Ask for ₩10,000 notes so market stalls can make change. Cash really only goes on market food, stalls and topping up a transport card.

## Where to exchange

- City money changers usually beat city banks, and city banks usually beat the airport.
- At the airport, change only enough to reach your hotel. Do the rest in the city.
- To withdraw cash with a foreign card, look for machines marked "Global ATM". Expect a machine fee (often ₩2,000 or more) on top of your own bank's fee.

## When your card is rejected

Korean apps turning down foreign cards is usually not your card. Many shops sign domestic-only payment contracts. A few things got better in 2026:

- **Baemin (food delivery):** Apple Pay with foreign Visa, Mastercard, JCB and Amex since June; since July you can order with a foreign card and no Korean phone number.
- **Naver:** since June, passport verification unlocks bookings, ordering and payment in Naver Map.
- **Taxis:** if your card keeps failing, Kakao Mobility's app for visitors, k.ride, takes overseas cards.
- **Subway:** since 17 March, new machines at 273 stations on Seoul Lines 1–8 sell Climate Card short-term passes and single tickets to foreign cards (about 3.7% fee).

## Is WOWPASS worth it?

It is a prepaid card for visitors that combines currency exchange, card payments and T-money. The card costs ₩5,000 (₩4,500 if you reserve in the app), and the official guide lists no fee for loading foreign cash or paying. Cash withdrawals are won only, up to ₩100,000 at a time, with a ₩1,000 fee. Worth it if you will exchange cash often; skip it if you will pay by card anyway.

## Leftover won

Spend your coins before you fly; changing won back at home costs you. A WOWPASS balance comes back as won in cash only.
