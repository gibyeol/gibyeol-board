## How we keep guides current

- Every guide shows the date it was last checked against official sources.
- Each guide ends with a **Report an update** button. It opens an email with the page address already filled in. Tell us what changed and, if you can, where you saw it.
- We re-check reports against the official source before changing anything, and log the change below.
