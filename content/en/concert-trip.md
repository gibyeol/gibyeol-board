## The one-line judgment

| Situation | What to do | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Buying the ticket | Use the global site named in the agency's notice (often NOL World) | Korean sites ask for Korean ID checks; global sites take your passport |
| Someone offers you a resale ticket | Walk away | Pickup is for the booker with matching ID, and markup reselling has been illegal since 28 August 2026 |
| Concert at KSPO Dome | Stay in Gangnam or Jamsil | Olympic Park Station (Lines 5 and 9) is about 7 minutes' walk away |
| Concert at Gocheok Sky Dome | Stay somewhere with an easy ride on Line 1 | Guil Station on Line 1 sits next to the dome, in Guro, southwest Seoul |
| Concert at Inspire Arena | Fit it around your flight | It's about 15 minutes by car from Incheon Airport, but 1 to 1.5 hours by taxi from central Seoul |
| Your suitcase | Leave it at the hotel | We could not confirm luggage storage you can count on at any venue |

What we left out: fan club membership how-tos (they change per agency and per tour), seat maps, and ticket prices. Who doesn't need this page: anyone going with a travel package that already includes the ticket and a shuttle.

## Buying tickets as a foreigner

**Find the right site first.** Each concert sells on one platform, chosen by the promoter. Check the agency's official notice before anything else. Korea JoongAng Daily lists global versions of Yes24 Ticket, Ticketlink, Interpark Ticket and Melon Ticket for people who don't live in Korea, and notes you need a separate account on the global site even if you have a Korean one.

**Interpark Global is now NOL World.** The foreigner ticket site run by NOL Universe became NOL World on 1 December 2025, at [world.nol.com](https://world.nol.com/en). Old guides that say "Global Interpark" mean this site. NOL World also sells city-to-venue shuttles and concert-plus-hotel packages for some shows.

**What one real listing asks of you.** A November 2026 fan meeting on NOL World is typical of what to expect:

- Verify your passport on your NOL World account before buying ("Verified Member Only").
- Collect tickets on site with a physical ID and your booking confirmation. The ID must match the booking and you must be the person who booked, or pickup is refused.
- Limits of 2 tickets per person in the fan club presale and 4 in total.
- Bookings made with macros or other abnormal methods can be cancelled.

Rules differ by show, so read the "ticket pickup" section of your own listing. Our call: book in the exact romanized name on your passport, and bring the passport itself, not a photo.

Card declined at checkout? That is a money problem, not a ticket problem. See [money](/money/).

## Resale and the 2026 law

The amended Performance Act took effect on **28 August 2026** (The Fact, 14 September 2026). In short:

- Reselling tickets above the price paid without the seller's consent, habitually or as a business, is illegal whether or not a macro was used.
- Buying tickets for resale by getting around the fair process (other people's accounts, macros) is also banned.
- Penalties: up to 1 year in prison or ₩10 million, and a surcharge of up to 50 times the sale amount for illegal resale.

The law targets sellers. For you, the practical risk is simpler: a ticket booked in someone else's name may not get you through pickup.

## The venues

**KSPO Dome (Olympic Park, Songpa).** About 15,000 seats for concerts. Take Line 5 or 9 to Olympic Park Station, Exit 4, and walk about 7 minutes. On Line 5, board a train toward Macheon, because the line splits. After the show, NOL World suggests walking to Hanseong Baekje (Line 9, about 20 minutes) or Mongchontoseong (Line 8, about 23 minutes) to dodge the crowd at Olympic Park.

**Gocheok Sky Dome (Guro).** About 20,000 for concerts, and it shares its calendar with pro baseball. Take Line 1 to Guil Station. The west exit opened in 2016 cut the walk to a few minutes. While you wait, the Seoul Art Book Pavilion on the dome's B1 level and the food street by Exit 2 are your shelter.

**Inspire Arena (Yeongjong Island, Incheon).** About 15,000 seats, roughly 15 minutes by car from the airport. Free resort shuttles run from Terminal 1 (stops 2C or 14C) and Terminal 2 (8C). They hold about 40 people, first come, first served, and fill up on event days. Some promoters run paid shuttles from Seoul, so check your concert's notice. A taxi to central Seoul takes about 1 to 1.5 hours.

**Seoul World Cup Stadium (Sangam).** More than 66,000 seats, but ground-level tickets are usually not sold, to protect the pitch. It is on Line 6 (World Cup Stadium Station).

**Jamsil Olympic Main Stadium.** Its three-year renovation ends at the end of 2026, then it hosts the LG Twins and Doosan Bears through 2031. No concerts have been announced there yet (Korea Times, 15 September 2026). If an old guide sends you to Jamsil for a stadium show, double-check.

## Your suitcase

NOL World lists lockers at Olympic Park Station but gives no sizes or prices, and we found nothing official for the other venues. Big bags are a burden in a standing crowd and on a packed train. Check out late, or leave your bag at the hotel and come back for it. If your flight is the same day, Inspire Arena is the only venue where that plan is easy.

## Getting back after the encore

Thousands of people head for the same station at once. Two habits help: check your last train in Naver Map before the show, not after, and know your backup. Last trains, night buses (N routes) and taxi apps are on [getting around Seoul](/getting-around/). Late-night taxi fares carry a surcharge of up to 40%.

## Where to stay, by venue

- **KSPO Dome:** Gangnam or Jamsil, the same side of the river. See [where to stay in Seoul](/seoul-areas/).
- **Gocheok Sky Dome:** not Gangnam, which is the wrong side of the city. Anywhere with an easy ride to Line 1 works. Check the door-to-door time in Naver Map before booking.
- **Seoul World Cup Stadium:** Hongdae is the nearest of the areas we cover.
- **Inspire Arena:** the resort itself or near the airport, especially if you fly the next morning.
