## The short answer

| Situation | What to do | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Any international flight from Incheon | Be at the terminal **3 hours** before departure | That is what Incheon Airport itself advises. It also warns that coming much earlier only adds to the crowd |
| Hotel near Seoul Station, flight in the afternoon or evening | Check in and clear immigration at **Seoul Station**, then take the AREX Express | Your bags are gone before you board the train, and you use a separate departure lane at Incheon |
| You have a refund slip (receipt over ₩1,000,000, or a shop without instant refund) | Run it through the refund kiosk in the departure hall **before** you check that bag | Customs may ask to see the goods, unused, so they cannot already be in the hold |

What we left out: Smart Pass, lounges and duty-free shopping. They are nice, not necessary. Who can skip most of this page: anyone flying out of Gimpo (see the end), and anyone whose tax refunds were all taken off at the till.

## Step 1: know your terminal

Incheon has two terminals, and they are not a short walk apart. The airport's own list, as of November 2026:

- **Terminal 2:** Korean Air, Asiana Airlines, Jin Air, Air Busan, Air Seoul, Delta, Air France, KLM, China Airlines and a few others.
- **Terminal 1:** Jeju Air, Trinity Airways (formerly T'way), Eastar Jet, Air Premia, Parata Air, Peach, ZIPAIR, EVA Air, Tigerair Taiwan, and most other foreign airlines.

Asiana moved from Terminal 1 to Terminal 2 on 14 January 2026, so older blog posts are wrong on this. Codeshares can also put you in a different terminal from the airline on your ticket. The airport says to check the terminal on your e-ticket or by searching your flight on airport.kr.

On AREX, Terminal 1 comes first and Terminal 2 is the last stop, so do not get off early.

## Step 2: how early, really

Incheon Airport's FAQ says to arrive about three hours before departure, and during the record Chuseok rush in September 2026 it repeated the same three hours while warning that very early arrivals make the queues worse. Boarding starts about 30 minutes before departure and the gate closes 10 minutes before. The airport asks you to be at the gate 30 to 40 minutes before boarding.

Before you leave the hotel, open **Expected Airport Congestion** on airport.kr. It forecasts passengers per hour at each terminal for the coming days, updated daily at 17:00, with colour levels from blue to red. If your departure hour shows orange or red, add time. There is also a separate real-time wait-times page.

For the ride itself, see [Incheon Airport to Seoul](/airport-to-seoul/). The same trains and limousine buses run the other way.

## Step 3: Seoul Station city airport check-in

This still works in 2026, and the area was renovated in August 2026 with a numbered queue and bag scales. You check in, drop bags and clear departure immigration on B2 of Seoul Station, then ride the AREX Express nonstop to the airport and use a dedicated departure entrance on the 3rd floor.

- **Hours:** check-in 05:20 to 19:00 (last intake 18:50), immigration 05:30 to 19:00.
- **Deadline:** finish check-in at least 3 hours before departure for Terminal 1 flights, 3 hours 20 minutes for Terminal 2.
- **Ticket:** you need an AREX **Express** ticket. The all-stop train does not count.
- **Airlines:** Seoul lists Korean Air, Asiana, Jeju Air, T'way, Air Seoul, Air Busan, Eastar Jet and Jin Air, but some routes are excluded. Korean Air and Asiana both exclude charters, codeshares flown by another airline, oversized bags and anyone needing assistance. Check your airline before you count on it.

Our call: if your flight leaves after lunch and you are staying near Seoul Station, Hongdae or the line to it, this is the best thing you can do on your last day.

## Step 4: tax refunds at the airport

Many shops now take the tax off at the till, for receipts up to ₩1,000,000. If all of yours did, there is nothing to do at the airport. If you have a refund slip, here is the order Incheon Airport gives:

1. **Goods in your carry-on:** check in first, then go to the refund kiosk in the departure hall.
2. **Goods in your checked bag:** tell the check-in agent. Your bag gets a customs tag and comes back to you. Run the kiosk, then put the bag on the large belt next to the customs desk.
3. If the kiosk asks for an inspection, take your passport, the unused goods and the receipt to the customs desk.
4. After security, refund kiosks and desks are in the duty-free area: **Terminal 1** near Gate 28 (desk 07:00–22:00), plus a kiosk in the Concourse by Gate 115; **Terminal 2** near Gate 253, across from Gate 250 (desk 07:00–21:30). Kiosks run 24 hours and pay cash, or a card refund for larger amounts.

Goods have to leave Korea within three months of purchase. Full rules: [Tax refunds in Korea](/tax-refund/).

## Step 5: power banks

Korea pushed for the new global rule, and ICAO adopted it from April 2026. What applies leaving Korea now:

- **Carry-on only.** Never in checked bags.
- **Up to two per person**, each 160 Wh or less. Between 100 and 160 Wh needs airline approval. Over 160 Wh is not allowed.
- **No charging onboard:** you may not charge the power bank from the seat, or charge your phone from the power bank.
- **Not in the overhead bin.** Keep it in the seat pocket or under the seat. Airlines hand out insulation tape at check-in, security and the gate if you want to cover the terminals.

Korean Air, Asiana, Jin Air, Air Busan, Air Seoul and Jeju Air already banned in-flight use in January 2026. Your airline can be stricter, so check its page.

## Step 6: liquids and the cosmetics you bought

- **Carry-on:** containers of 100 ml or less, in one clear resealable bag of up to 1 litre per person. Kimchi and gochujang count as liquids too.
- **Over 100 ml from Olive Young or a market:** put it in your checked bag.
- **Duty-free liquids** bought after security go in the sealed duty-free bag and can be any size. Keep it sealed until your last flight, which matters if you connect somewhere.
- **Sprays in checked bags:** non-flammable only, up to 500 ml each and 2 litres in total.

## Step 7: leftover won

Spend the coins first, on a drink or a convenience-store snack, because changing won back at home costs you. If you used WOWPASS, the balance comes back only as won in cash, so draw it down before the airport. More in [Money in Korea](/money/).

## Flying from Gimpo instead

Some short international flights, including to Japan, leave from Gimpo, not Incheon. ANA, for example, flies Gimpo to Tokyo Haneda daily. Check the airport code on your ticket: **GMP** is Gimpo, **ICN** is Incheon. Seoul Station check-in covers Incheon departures only.
