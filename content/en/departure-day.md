## Your last morning

Here is the last morning we would plan, built from the airport's own procedures. If you stay near Seoul Station, go down to B2 of the station: check in, drop your bags and clear passport control there, then ride the AREX Express nonstop to the airport. At Incheon you walk in through a dedicated departure entrance on the 3rd floor, your suitcase already in the system.

After security and passport control there is no going back, so finish the landside jobs first. Airside, pick up any duty-free order and run any leftover refund, then walk to your gate. Our pick for the ending: your last coins spent on a drink, and you at the gate 30–40 minutes before boarding, which is what the airport asks.

{{fig:departure-day-1|Departure at Incheon, in order}}

## The short answer

| Situation | What to do | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Any international flight from Incheon | Be at the terminal **3 hours** before departure | The airport's own advice; it also warns that much earlier only adds to the crowd |
| Hotel near Seoul Station, flight after lunch | Check in at **Seoul Station**, then take the AREX Express | Bags gone before you board the train, and a separate lane at Incheon |
| You have a refund slip | Run it at the refund kiosk **before** that bag is checked | Customs may ask to see the goods, unused |
| You ordered duty-free online | Collect it at the counter on your voucher, after passport control | Goods are handed over only on departure |

What we left out: Smart Pass and lounges. Nice, not necessary. Who can skip most of this page: anyone flying from Gimpo (see the end), and anyone whose tax refunds were all taken off at the till.

## Step 1: know your terminal

Incheon's two terminals are not a short walk apart. The airport's list, as of November 2026:

- **Terminal 2:** Korean Air, Asiana Airlines, Jin Air, Air Busan, Air Seoul, Delta, Air France, KLM, China Airlines and a few others.
- **Terminal 1:** Jeju Air, Trinity Airways (formerly T'way), Eastar Jet, Air Premia, Parata Air, Peach, ZIPAIR, EVA Air, Tigerair Taiwan and most other foreign airlines.

Asiana moved to Terminal 2 on 14 January 2026, so older posts are wrong. Codeshares can put you in a different terminal; check your e-ticket or search your flight on airport.kr.

{{fig:departure-day-2|Which terminal, and where it is on the AREX}}

On AREX, Terminal 1 comes first and Terminal 2 is the last stop, so do not get off early.

## Step 2: how early, really

Three hours, says Incheon Airport, and during the September 2026 Chuseok rush it repeated that while warning that very early arrivals make queues worse. Boarding starts about 30 minutes before departure and the gate closes 10 minutes before.

Before you leave the hotel, open **Expected Airport Congestion** on airport.kr. It forecasts passengers per hour at each terminal, updated daily at 17:00, coloured blue to red. If your hour shows orange or red, add time. For the ride, see [Incheon Airport to Seoul](/airport-to-seoul/); the same trains and buses run the other way.

## Step 3: Seoul Station city airport check-in

If your flight leaves after lunch and you are staying near Seoul Station, Hongdae or the line to it, this is the best thing you can do on your last day. The area was renovated in August 2026 with a numbered queue and bag scales.

{{fig:departure-day-3|Seoul Station check-in: hours and deadlines}}

- **Where and when:** B2 of Seoul Station. Check-in and bag drop 05:20–19:00 (last intake 18:50), immigration 05:30–19:00.
- **Deadline:** 3 hours before departure for Terminal 1 flights, 3 hours 20 minutes for Terminal 2.
- **Ticket:** an AREX **Express** ticket. The all-stop train does not count.
- **Flights:** same-day international departures from Incheon, and only some airlines. Korean Air and Asiana exclude charters, codeshares flown by another airline, oversized bags and anyone needing assistance. Check your airline first.

## Step 4: tax refunds at the airport

Many shops take the tax off at the till for receipts under ₩1,000,000. If all of yours did, skip this. With a refund slip, Incheon Airport's order is:

1. **Goods in your carry-on:** check in first, then use the refund kiosk in the departure hall.
2. **Goods in your checked bag:** tell the check-in agent. The bag gets a customs tag and comes back to you. Run the kiosk, then put the bag on the large belt next to the customs desk.
3. If the kiosk asks for an inspection, take your passport, the unused goods and the receipt to the customs desk.
4. After security there are more kiosks and desks: **Terminal 1** near Gate 28 (desk 07:00–22:00) and in the Concourse by Gate 115; **Terminal 2** near Gate 253, across from Gate 250 (desk 07:00–21:30). The kiosks run 24 hours and pay cash, or a card refund for larger amounts.

Goods must leave Korea within three months of purchase. Full rules: [Tax refunds in Korea](/tax-refund/).

## Step 5: duty-free pickup

If you ordered online or downtown, the goods wait at the counter named on your voucher, after passport control. Bring your passport and boarding pass. Hyundai asks you to come at least an hour before departure.

{{fig:departure-day-4|After passport control: refunds and pickup counters}}

- **Terminal 1 West:** 4th floor, across from Gate 43. **Concourse:** near Gate 119 (4th floor), after the shuttle train.
- **Terminal 2:** near Gate 225 (West) and towards Gate 274 (East), 2nd floor.

More in [Duty-free in Korea](/duty-free/).

## Step 6: what goes in which bag

Korea pushed for the new power bank rule, and ICAO adopted it; it applies from 20 April 2026.

{{fig:departure-day-5|What goes in which bag}}

- **Power banks:** carry-on only, never checked. Up to two per person, each 160 Wh or less (100–160 Wh needs airline approval). No charging them from the seat or charging your phone from them in flight. Not in the overhead bin: keep them in the seat pocket or under the seat. Airlines hand out insulating tape at check-in, security and the gate if you want to cover the terminals.
- **Liquids in carry-on:** 100 ml or less per container, in one clear resealable bag of up to 1 litre. Kimchi and gochujang count.
- **Over 100 ml** (Olive Young, a market): checked bag.
- **Duty-free liquids** bought after security come in a sealed bag of any size. Keep it sealed until your last flight.
- **Sprays in checked bags:** non-flammable only, up to 500 ml each and 2 litres in total.

Korean Air, Asiana, Jin Air, Air Busan, Air Seoul and Jeju Air already banned in-flight use in January 2026. Your airline can be stricter; check its page.

## Step 7: leftover won

Spend the coins first, on a drink or a convenience-store snack, because changing won at home costs you. WOWPASS balances come back only as won in cash, so draw it down before the airport. More in [Money in Korea](/money/).

## Flying from Gimpo instead

Some short international flights, including to Japan, leave from Gimpo. ANA, for example, flies Gimpo to Tokyo Haneda daily. **GMP** on your ticket is Gimpo, **ICN** is Incheon. Seoul Station check-in covers Incheon only.
