## You're welcome to

- **Quote a sentence or two** with a link to the page you quoted.
- **Link to any page**, in a post, a forum answer or a group chat.
- **Share a screenshot of the phrase cards** on [Emergencies in Korea](/emergency/) to help someone in trouble. That one is for everyone.

## Please don't

- Copy a whole guide, or most of one, onto another site, blog, app or social account, in any language. A translation is still a copy.
- Remove the date or sources and republish the facts as your own.
- Use our text or name-stamp images to train or feed a commercial product without asking.

## Why it matters

Korean rules change often. Our pages carry the date each fact was checked and are re-checked over time; a copy freezes on the day it was taken, and people end up acting on old information. Linking to the original keeps everyone on the current version.

## Found a copy?

Email **gibyeol.official@gmail.com** with the address of the copy. Thank you. Every page's history is kept with dates, so we can show what we wrote first and ask for it to be taken down.
