<details class="tr-more"><summary><i class="ic ic-percent" aria-hidden="true"></i>Why is it big? More</summary>

- **+718%:** sales in Musinsa's 'lightweight puffer' category in August 2026, compared with the same month a year before (Musinsa data, Korea Herald 2026-10-20). Sales volume more than 8 times (a Musinsa official). *Nadri's reading: we read it as people already buying a lot in August, before the cold.*
- **Now a main item:** from an extra jacket for in-between seasons or the outdoors → a main fall and winter item. This year: shaped silhouettes with a waistline, roomy shoulders, oatmeal and fog gray (same article).
- **Pale colors:** more interest in pastels than in strong primary colors, black or gray (Korea Herald 2026-11-23, no numbers).
- **First stock sold out (Oct 20 article):** Musinsa Standard City Leisure Hooded Light Down (Cloudy Blue, all sizes; No. 1 in sales, but the article does not say which ranking) · New Balance Flying 77 (within a day of launch) · The North Face Ventus On (about 40 minutes in 2025; Real Black and Black, all sizes, in 2026) · MLB Curve Air Shell in lavender (some restocked).

</details>
<details class="tr-more"><summary><i class="ic ic-scan-line" aria-hidden="true"></i>More on size</summary>

1. Lay a puffer that fits you well flat, measure the chest width and the total length, and compare with the size chart of the one you want to buy.
2. If you will wear it under a coat, measure the coat too and check the puffer is not bigger. Sizes are different for each brand.

</details>
<details class="tr-more"><summary><i class="ic ic-list-checks" aria-hidden="true"></i>More on label words</summary>

- **Fill power:** how much the down puffs up and traps air. The higher it is, the warmer for the same weight. For sleeping bags, 600–650 is good, 700–750 very good, 800 or more the best. Warmth also depends on how much down is inside (Wikipedia 'Fill power'). Example: Arc'teryx Cerium, 850 fill.
- **Down : feather:** the down ratio on the label. The higher the fill power, the bigger the share of down (Wikipedia).
- **Synthetic fill:** Arc'teryx says it uses synthetic fill (Coreloft, 100% polyester) in spots that get damp easily.
- **Fit:** Arc'teryx describes its trim (fitted) fit as good for layering.
- **Packable:** folds into a storage pouch (Arc'teryx Cerium).
- Words we have not found a usable source for yet: water-repellent (DWR), the difference between duck down and goose down, and where 'lightweight' ends and 'midweight' starts.

</details>
