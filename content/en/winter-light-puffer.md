<details class="tr-more"><summary><i class="ic ic-scan-line" aria-hidden="true"></i>More on size</summary>

1. With a tape measure, measure the widest part just under your armpits → find the box in the chart above (Uniqlo body sizes, Genderless Light Down Volume Jacket).
2. Lay a puffer that fits you well flat, measure the chest width and the total length, and compare with the product sizes below.
3. If you will wear it under a coat, measure the coat too and check the puffer is not bigger. Uniqlo describes Ultra Light Down as "a regular fit you can wear as outerwear, and also as an inner layer under other outerwear". Sizes are different for each brand.

<details class="tr-more"><summary><i class="ic ic-list-checks" aria-hidden="true"></i>More on label words</summary>

- **Fill power:** how much the down puffs up and traps air. The higher it is, the warmer for the same weight. For sleeping bags, 600–650 is good, 700–750 very good, 800 or more the best. Warmth also depends on how much down is inside (Wikipedia 'Fill power'). Example: Arc'teryx Cerium, 850 fill.
- **Down : feather:** the down ratio on the label. The higher the fill power, the bigger the share of down (Wikipedia). Uniqlo Ultra Light Down 90:10, Light Down Volume 85:15 (recycled down).
- **Synthetic fill:** Uniqlo PUFFTECH uses polyester fill. Arc'teryx says it uses synthetic fill (Coreloft) in spots that get damp easily.
- **Fit:** Uniqlo labels slim, regular (standard) and oversized (roomy). Of these, the one marked "can also be worn as an inner layer under other outerwear" is the regular fit (Ultra Light Down). Arc'teryx calls its trim (fitted) fit "a fit good for layering".
- **Packable:** folds into a storage pouch (Uniqlo Ultra Light Down, Arc'teryx Cerium).
- **Water-repellent (DWR):** a finish that makes light drops bead off. It does not last forever and gets weaker with washing (Uniqlo).
- Words we have not found a source for yet: the difference between duck down and goose down, and where 'lightweight' ends and 'midweight' starts.

</details>
