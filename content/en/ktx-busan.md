## Your Busan day, from the platform

You booked this seat weeks ago, because tickets now open two months before departure. At Seoul Station you board the KTX; we expect the fastest trains to reach Busan in about 2 hours 17 minutes and typical ones in roughly 2 hours 35 to 41 (based on 2024 reporting; see How long it takes below). Busan Station sits on subway Line 1, and Chinatown starts right by Exit 1. Our pick for the ending scene: hand your bag to the staffed storage desk on the station's first floor, which can deliver it to hotels in Haeundae and Gwangalli, and walk out with your hands free.

## The short answer

| Your situation | What to do | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Seoul to Busan and back, few other trains | Buy two regular tickets in the Korail+ app or on Korail's website for visitors | The standard fare is ₩54,400 each way, so a round trip (₩108,800) costs less than the cheapest Korail Pass (₩131,000) |
| Three or more long train rides packed into a few days | Price out the Korail Pass | It only wins when your regular fares add up to more than the pass |
| You want to pay in your own currency | Book on Klook | It has sold live Korail seats since April 2026 |
| Travelling around Seollal (6–9 February 2027) | Book the day holiday sales open, or move your dates | Holiday trains are sold in a separate sale and the busiest days fill fast |

How we chose: the trip most visitors actually make, the cheapest way to book it, and the one pass question. What we left out: sleeper-style tourist trains and regional lines.

<h2 id="what-changed-in-2026">When does KTX booking open? What changed in 2026</h2>

Three things, and older guides miss all of them:

- **Booking opens two months ahead.** Since 1 October 2026, KTX and other rail tickets go on sale two months before departure instead of one. This applies to foreigners too: the transport ministry and Korail said the point was to let visitors book trains at the same time as their flights.
- **KTX and SRT merged.** Since 1 September 2026 they run as one network, and both are booked in one app, Korail+. That app is the old KorailTalk, renamed and updated. If you have KorailTalk on your phone, just update it.
- **Fares went down.** The Seoul–Busan standard fare dropped from ₩59,800 to ₩54,400 on 1 September 2026, a 10% cut to match SRT fares.

<h2 id="where-to-book-as-a-visitor">How do I book KTX tickets as a foreigner?</h2>

From two months before departure, in the Korail+ app or on Korail's website for visitors, paying with an overseas credit card. Step by step:

1. **Check the date.** Tickets go on sale two months before departure.
2. **Open the app or the website.** The Korail+ app lists English, Japanese, Simplified and Traditional Chinese, Vietnamese and Thai. Korail's website for visitors ([English](https://www.korail.com/global/eng/main)) runs in seven languages, including English, Chinese, Japanese, Thai and Vietnamese, with French, German and Mongolian planned by the end of 2026. The old address, letskorail.com, now forwards to korail.com (we checked on 27 November 2026).
3. **Pay.** Overseas credit cards such as Visa, Mastercard and UnionPay are accepted. If your card fails, Klook (below) is the fallback.
4. **Get your ticket.** Tickets bought in Korail+ appear under "My Ticket", as a ticket list and a ticket screen. The app can also save a ticket to your photos.
5. **Your train is sold out?** Search the same Korail+ app for trains from Suseo Station (the former SRT). Since the September 2026 merger, both are booked in one app.
6. **Turning up at Seoul Station without a booking?** It has dedicated ticket counters with real-time AI interpretation, and they have taken Apple Pay since April 2025 (Korea Tourism Organization, May 2025).

We could not confirm on Korail's own pages whether you need to sign up as a member, or where your passport name goes, so we left those out.

{{fig:ktx-busan-1|Booking a KTX: when sales open, where to buy, what to choose}}

Five places sell tickets:

- **Korail+ app.** This is where Koreans book, so it shows everything.
- **Korail's website for visitors.** Step 2 above. Typing the old letskorail.com address takes you to korail.com.
- **Klook.** Since 20 April 2026 it has sold real-time Korail tickets with live seat availability, in about 20 languages and 40 currencies. Handy if your card struggles with Korean checkouts.
- **At the station.** As of late 2025, Korail had new ticket machines at 148 stations that take overseas credit cards.
- **With your flight.** Since June 2026 some partner airlines and travel agencies sell AREX and KTX tickets together with a flight ("Rail&Air"). You cannot buy that combination in Korail+, on letskorail.com or at a station counter.

When you pick a seat, look at its direction: depending on the train, seats face forward or backward, and you can choose. The government says it is developing a system to take global mobile payments such as WeChat Pay and Alipay. There was no launch date when we checked.

## How long it takes

The fastest KTX trains run Seoul to Busan in about 2 hours 17 minutes; typical ones take roughly 2 hours 35 to 41 minutes. Those times come from 2024 reporting and Korail says big time savings won't come before 2028, so we expect about the same today. Check the exact time when you book.

## Which Seoul station: Seoul, Suseo or Cheongnyangni

SRT trains leave from **Suseo** in southeast Seoul, not Seoul Station. If you are staying in Gangnam or Jamsil, that can save you a cross-town ride. Since the merger, you book them in the same Korail+ app.

{{fig:ktx-busan-2|Three Seoul stations with trains to Busan}}

One catch: the Korail Pass did not cover SRT as of August 2026, and we could not confirm whether that changed after the merger. If you hold a pass, leave from Seoul or Yongsan unless Korail tells you otherwise.

<h2 id="is-the-korail-pass-worth-it">Is the Korail Pass worth it? How many rides to break even</h2>

It is a pass for foreigners only, sold as 2, 3, 4 or 5 travel days used within a validity of up to 10 days. Adult prices reported in December 2025 were ₩131,000, ₩186,000, ₩234,000 and ₩275,000. It covers standard cars on Korail trains, with up to two seat reservations a day. First class costs an extra 50% of the first-class fare. Subway and temporary tourist trains are excluded.

{{fig:ktx-busan-3|One Seoul–Busan round trip against the Korail Pass, adult}}

Our call: do the maths before you buy. One Seoul–Busan round trip doesn't reach the price of a 2-day pass. The pass makes sense only if you are covering several long legs in a short window. Prices for youth and group passes are on Korail's website for visitors; we didn't verify them here.

Count your one-way Seoul–Busan rides. If the total below is more than the pass you're looking at, the pass wins. Compare against the price on the checkout screen just before you buy.

| Seoul–Busan standard, one way | Regular fares added up (our maths: ₩54,400 × rides) |
|---|---|
| 2 (one round trip) | ₩108,800 |
| 3 | ₩163,200 |
| 4 (two round trips) | ₩217,600 |
| 5 | ₩272,000 |
| 6 (three round trips) | ₩326,400 |

The pass lets you reserve seats up to twice a day, so an N-day pass covers at most 2×N reserved rides (our maths: four on a 2-day pass). We couldn't confirm fares for other routes from an official source, so they're not in the table.

**Where to buy it:** on Korail's website or at train stations. The Korail+ app also sells rail passes under "Travel Service Reservation".

{{aff:klook_korailpass|KORAIL PASS on Klook (2–5 days)}}

**Using the pass, step by step** (as Klook's product page describes it):

1. After buying, save the pass e-ticket on your phone or print it.
2. For each ride, reserve a seat ticket on Korail's website or app. You must enter the same passport you used to buy the pass.
3. Print the seat ticket or save its QR code, and show it at the station.

Conditions on the same page: not available to Korean passport holders; ages 13 and up pay the adult rate; one infant aged 0–5 rides free on the lap of a traveller over 13, but a separate seat needs a child ticket; one pass per passport. Klook says confirmation comes within minutes.

Klook's page says "unlimited access to all trains, including the high-speed KTX". The Korea Tourism Organization's guide says SRT, the subway and temporary tourist trains are excluded and seat reservations are limited to two a day. Go by the official rules.

## Seollal 2027

Seollal falls on Sunday 7 February 2027, and the holiday runs 6–9 February. Korean holiday trains are sold in a separate sale; for Seollal 2026 it ran in mid-January. On the busiest day leaving Seoul in 2026, 87.1% of seats sold in that sale. Korail had not announced the 2027 dates when we checked. If you can, travel to Busan before the 6th or after the 9th. What else closes over the holiday is in our [winter guide](/winter/).

## Landing in Busan instead

Gimhae Airport is Busan's international airport, so you don't have to start in Seoul.

{{fig:ktx-busan-4|From Gimhae Airport into Busan}}

- **Light rail** is the cheapest and fastest way into the city. Change to the Busan Metro at Sasang (Line 2) or Daejeo (Line 3). Busan Station is on Line 1; your map app will show you where to change.
- **Airport Limousine 2** runs to Seomyeon and Bujeon for ₩6,000 (adult), about 50 minutes, every 60–80 minutes.
- **Airport Limousine 1** runs to Haeundae for ₩9,500, from the first-floor bus stop.
- Limousines are cashless. Buy at the machine on the 1st floor by Gate 4, or tap a card on board if seats are left. Two bags up to 30 kg in total ride free.

## Busan basics

- **Busan Station** is on subway Line 1. Chinatown starts right by Exit 1.
- **Staying in Haeundae?** KTX-Eum trains from Cheongnyangni in eastern Seoul now stop at Sinhaeundae and Centum. They take longer (about 3 hours 38 minutes to Bujeon), but they drop you on the right side of the city.
- **Luggage.** Busan Station has a staffed storage desk on the 1st floor that can also deliver bags to hotels in Haeundae and Gwangalli. We couldn't confirm prices. The Korail+ app also sells luggage delivery as an add-on.
- **Buses.** Since 1 October 2026, Busan has been testing cashless city buses on 35 routes for three months. Carry a transport card (see [essentials](/essentials/)) and you won't notice.
- **Cash and cards:** the same rules apply as in Seoul. See [money](/money/).

## Before you go

1. Two months before your travel date, book: tickets go on sale then.
2. Match the station to your hotel: Seoul or Yongsan, Suseo for Gangnam and Jamsil, Cheongnyangni for KTX-Eum to Haeundae.
3. Add up your long rides before you look at the pass.
4. Travelling over Seollal? Move the trip off 6–9 February if you can.
