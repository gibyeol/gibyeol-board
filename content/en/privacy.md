## What Nadri collects

Nadri has no accounts, forms or newsletter, and sets no cookies of its own.

## Services that see your visit

- **Hosting.** The site is served by Cloudflare, which processes standard request data such as your IP address to deliver pages and block abuse.
- **Fonts.** Headings use a typeface loaded from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google's servers when a page loads.
- **Affiliate partners.** When you click an affiliate link, the partner site (for example Klook or Coupang) may set cookies to credit the referral. Their own privacy policies apply once you are on their site.
