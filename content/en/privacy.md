## What Nadri collects

Nadri has no accounts, forms or newsletter, and sets no cookies of its own.

## Services that see your visit

- **Hosting.** The site is served by Cloudflare, which processes standard request data such as your IP address to deliver pages and block abuse.
- **Fonts.** Fonts are served from this site itself, so no font service sees your visit.
- **Visit statistics.** Cloudflare Web Analytics counts page views, referring sites and country without cookies and without building a profile of you. Nadri sees totals only.
- **Affiliate link script.** Pages load a script from Travelpayouts (our travel affiliate network) that turns some travel links into affiliate links and measures clicks on them. It may store a small identifier in your browser to credit bookings.
- **Shopping affiliate script.** Shopping pages that show the affiliate notice also load a script from Skimlinks, which turns some store links into affiliate links and measures clicks on them. It may store a small identifier in your browser to credit purchases. Pages about health and emergencies do not load it.
- **Your choice.** These affiliate scripts load only after you tap "Accept" in the cookie notice. If you reject, or your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, they stay off. [Change your cookie choice](#consent-reset).
- **Affiliate partners.** When you click an affiliate link, the partner site (for example Klook, Agoda or Airalo) may set cookies to credit the referral. Their own privacy policies apply once you are on their site.
