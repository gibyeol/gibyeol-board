## What Nadri collects

Nadri has no accounts or newsletter, and sets no cookies of its own.

- **Feedback form.** If you send something with the "Tell us" form at the bottom of a page, we keep only what you typed, the type you picked, and which page (and language) you sent it from, in Nadri's private repository on GitHub. We do not ask for your name or email and do not keep your IP address. Please don't write personal details in it. To have something you sent deleted, write to the contact address below.

## Services that see your visit

- **Spam check.** When you open the feedback form, Cloudflare Turnstile runs a check in your browser to tell people from bots. Cloudflare states that Turnstile does not access, store or transmit form entries. Details: [Turnstile Privacy Addendum](https://www.cloudflare.com/turnstile-privacy-policy/).
- **Hosting.** The site is served by Cloudflare, which processes standard request data such as your IP address to deliver pages and block abuse.
- **Fonts.** Fonts are served from this site itself, so no font service sees your visit.
- **Visit statistics.** Cloudflare Web Analytics counts page views, referring sites and country without cookies and without building a profile of you. Nadri sees totals only.
- **Affiliate link script.** Only pages that show the affiliate notice (12 pages, such as the airport, eSIM and shopping pages), and only after you choose "Accept" in the cookie notice, load a script from Travelpayouts (our travel affiliate network) that turns some travel links into affiliate links and measures clicks on them. It may store a small identifier in your browser to credit bookings.
- **Shopping affiliate script.** The same 12 pages also load a script from Skimlinks, which turns some store links into affiliate links and measures clicks on them. It may store a small identifier in your browser to credit purchases. Pages about health and emergencies do not load it.
- **Your choice.** These affiliate scripts load only after you tap "Accept" in the cookie notice. If you reject, or your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, they stay off. [Change your cookie choice](#consent-reset).
- **Affiliate partners.** When you click an affiliate link, the affiliate network (for example Awin or Travelpayouts) and the partner site (for example Klook, Yesim or Stylevana) may record the click and set cookies to credit the referral. Their own privacy policies apply once you are on their site.
