## What Nadri is for

Planning a trip to Korea means piecing together entry forms, apps, payment quirks and sale calendars from a dozen places, half of them out of date. Nadri puts the answers in one place and keeps them current.

## How guides are made

- Each guide is researched against official sources first: government sites, airport operators and the stores themselves. They are listed at the end of every guide.
- We use AI tools to help gather information and draft text. Every fact is checked by an editor before publishing, and each page shows the date it was last checked.
- We never write as if we used a product or visited a place when we did not. Reviews on Nadri summarise what many buyers report, and say so.

## Corrections

If something on Nadri is wrong or out of date, email **gibyeol.official@gmail.com**. We fix confirmed errors and update the page date.

## Where we are going

Korea first: shopping, travel essentials, food and culture. Other destinations will follow, each with its own editors.
