## What Nadri is for

Planning a trip to Korea means piecing together entry forms, apps, payment quirks and sale calendars from a dozen places, some of them already out of date. Nadri puts the answers in one place and keeps them current.

## How guides are made

- Each guide is researched against official sources first: government sites, airport operators and the stores themselves. They are listed at the end of every guide.
- We use AI tools to help gather information and draft text. Each guide page shows, at the top, the date it was last checked against official information and whether a Nadri editor has approved that version (pages that are not guides, such as the home page, the name tool, the shopping hub and the copyright page, do not).
- We never write as if we used a product or visited a place when we did not. Reviews on Nadri summarise what many buyers report, and say so.

## Corrections

If something on Nadri is wrong or out of date, email **gibyeol.official@gmail.com**. We fix confirmed errors and update the page date.

## What we want Nadri to be

A good day out, for everyone in it. The traveller should spend less time decoding and more time enjoying. The shop, the restaurant and the neighbourhood should get visitors who arrive knowing what to expect. And the people who live here should meet guests who understand the small rules. When a guide helps all three, we think it has done its job.

So we hold ourselves to a few things: say what we think, not just what exists; show where every fact came from and when we checked it; and leave out what doesn't help you, even when it would earn us money.

Korea comes first: shopping, travel essentials, food and culture. Other destinations will follow, each with its own editors.

## Build it with us

Nadri gets better every time someone tells us what actually happened. You are part of how it's made:

- **Tell us what went wrong** on your trip: a rule that changed, a card that didn't work, a place that wasn't what we said. Your mistake becomes the next traveller's warning.
- **Tell us what we got right or missed**: the question you couldn't find answered here is the next guide we write.
- **Correct us.** If a fact is out of date, we would rather hear it today than find it next month.

Write to **gibyeol.official@gmail.com**. We read everything, we fix confirmed errors, and we date every change on our [Updates](/updates/) page. We never publish your name or details without asking.
