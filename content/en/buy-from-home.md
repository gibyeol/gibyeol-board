<h2 id="customs">Tax on arrival: one line for your country</h2>

| Your country | Little or no tax up to | Over the limit, or watch out |
|---|---|---|
| UK | £135 or less: no customs duty. The seller adds VAT to the price you pay ⑬ | Over £135: VAT + duty when it arrives ⑬ |
| Canada | Items mailed to you worth CAN$20 or less: no duty or tax ⑭⑮ | Over that: duty + GST/HST + PST on the full value ⑮ |
| Australia | AUD1,000 or less: generally nothing to pay at the border. The seller may charge GST at checkout ⑯ | Over that: Import Declaration + processing charge + duties and taxes ⑯ |
| Singapore | Each item S$400 or less: a GST-registered seller includes GST at checkout, and you don't pay it again on arrival ⑰ | Shipment over S$400 from a seller not registered for GST: GST on arrival ⑰ |
| Hong Kong | Customs collects duty on four kinds of goods: liquor, tobacco, certain hydrocarbon oil, methyl alcohol ⑲ | Cosmetics and clothes are not on this list of four ⑲ |
| EU | The exemption has ended. From 1 July 2026, parcels of €150 or less also pay €3 customs duty per type of item ㉑ | This €3 is customs duty. For VAT, see your country's customs guidance |
| Japan | Taxable value ¥10,000 or less: no customs duty or consumption tax. For personal use, the taxable value is what you paid × 0.6 ⑤⑥ | Knitwear, leather bags and leather shoes pay duty even under ¥10,000 ⑤. Up to 24 of the same cosmetic ⑨ |
| Taiwan (courier) | Courier parcel with a taxable value of NT$2,000 or less: no customs duty, business tax or commodity tax ⑳ | If you use this exemption more than 6 times in half a year, it no longer applies ⑳ |

The actual tax is decided by your country's customs and the delivery company when they see the goods. We opened and checked each country's official customs or government guidance on 26–27 November 2026. For countries not in the table (such as the US) and for cosmetics-only rules (other than Japan's), see that country's official customs guidance.</span></aside>

<details class="tr-more"><summary><i class="ic ic-map-pin" aria-hidden="true"></i>UK in detail</summary>

<ul class="tr-choose"><li><i class="ic ic-circle-check" aria-hidden="true"></i><b>£135 or less: no duty, VAT at checkout</b><span>If you bought the goods yourself and they are not excise goods (alcohol, tobacco, vapes), the seller includes VAT in what you pay. There is no customs duty ⑬.</span></li><li><i class="ic ic-triangle-alert" aria-hidden="true"></i><b>Over £135: VAT + duty on arrival</b><span>You pay VAT to the delivery company. Duty is charged on the price of the goods plus postage, packaging and insurance, and the rate depends on the type of goods and where they were made ⑬.</span></li><li><i class="ic ic-mail" aria-hidden="true"></i><b>How you pay</b><span>Royal Mail, Parcelforce or the courier contacts you with a bill. They normally hold the parcel for about 3 weeks; if you haven't paid by then, it goes back to the sender ⑬.</span></li><li><i class="ic ic-receipt" aria-hidden="true"></i><b>Sending it back, or paid too much</b><span>Claim a refund with form BOR 286 if Royal Mail or Parcelforce delivered, or form C285 for any other courier ⑬.</span></li><li><i class="ic ic-info" aria-hidden="true"></i><b>Northern Ireland is different</b><span>Goods treated as at risk of moving into the EU can cost €3 per item plus a €2 per item handling fee, even at £135 or less ⑬.</span></li></ul>

[GOV.UK: Tax and duty on goods sent from abroad](https://www.gov.uk/goods-sent-from-abroad/tax-and-duty)

</details>

<details class="tr-more"><summary><i class="ic ic-map-pin" aria-hidden="true"></i>Canada in detail</summary>

<ul class="tr-choose"><li><i class="ic ic-circle-check" aria-hidden="true"></i><b>Mail CAN$20 or less: no duty or tax</b><span>Over that, you pay duty, GST or HST and, depending on the province, PST on the full value ⑮.</span></li><li><i class="ic ic-ban" aria-hidden="true"></i><b>Not covered by the CAN$20 exemption</b><span>Tobacco, books, periodicals, magazines, alcohol, and goods ordered through a Canadian post office box or a Canadian intermediary ⑮.</span></li><li><i class="ic ic-mail" aria-hidden="true"></i><b>How you pay</b><span>If you owe anything, Form E14 comes with the parcel. Canada Post charges a handling fee on parcels that owe duty or tax ⑭. A courier may handle customs for you, so check its shipping and handling fees before you buy ⑭.</span></li></ul>

[CBSA: Importing by mail or courier](https://www.cbsa-asfc.gc.ca/import/courier/menu-eng.html)

</details>

<details class="tr-more"><summary><i class="ic ic-map-pin" aria-hidden="true"></i>Australia in detail</summary>

<ul class="tr-choose"><li><i class="ic ic-circle-check" aria-hidden="true"></i><b>AUD1,000 or less: usually nothing at the border</b><span>Since 1 July 2018, overseas sellers may charge GST at checkout instead. Tobacco and alcohol are taxed whatever the value ⑯.</span></li><li><i class="ic ic-triangle-alert" aria-hidden="true"></i><b>Over AUD1,000</b><span>You lodge an Import Declaration and pay a processing charge plus duties and taxes ⑯.</span></li><li><i class="ic ic-ban" aria-hidden="true"></i><b>Returning it doesn't refund the duty</b><span>If you send goods back because you changed your mind, they don't fit or you don't like them, the law doesn't allow a refund of the customs duty ⑯.</span></li></ul>

[ABF: Buying online](https://www.abf.gov.au/buying-online/buying-online)

</details>

<details class="tr-more"><summary><i class="ic ic-map-pin" aria-hidden="true"></i>Singapore in detail</summary>

<ul class="tr-choose"><li><i class="ic ic-tag" aria-hidden="true"></i><b>"S$400" means the item price only</b><span>Shipping, insurance and tax are left out. An item at S$395 + S$25 shipping counts as S$395 ⑰.</span></li><li><i class="ic ic-credit-card" aria-hidden="true"></i><b>GST-registered seller: once, at checkout</b><span>If each item is S$400 or less and comes by air or post, GST is in the price you pay and isn't charged again on arrival. Each item is looked at separately, even if the total is over S$400 ⑰.</span></li><li><i class="ic ic-package" aria-hidden="true"></i><b>Seller not registered: on arrival if the shipment is over S$400</b><span>Based on the total of goods + insurance + freight (CIF) ⑰.</span></li><li><i class="ic ic-smartphone" aria-hidden="true"></i><b>How you pay</b><span>Post: S$400 or less is simply delivered. Over that, the SingPost app sends a notification and you pay within 14 days in the app, at a SAM kiosk or at any post office ⑱. Courier: ask the courier how to pay; it may charge extra service fees for customs clearance ⑱.</span></li><li><i class="ic ic-receipt" aria-hidden="true"></i><b>Charged GST twice?</b><span>It can happen if the seller's GST details reach the courier too late. See "Refund of Taxes" on the Singapore Customs site ⑰.</span></li></ul>

[Singapore Customs: Buying from overseas](https://www.customs.gov.sg/personal-shipment/buying-from-overseas/)

</details>

<details class="tr-more"><summary><i class="ic ic-map-pin" aria-hidden="true"></i>Hong Kong in detail</summary>

<ul class="tr-choose"><li><i class="ic ic-info" aria-hidden="true"></i><b>Four kinds of dutiable goods</b><span>Hong Kong Customs collects duty on liquor, tobacco (except smokeless tobacco and alternative smoking products), certain hydrocarbon oil and methyl alcohol ⑲. Cosmetics and clothes are not on this list.</span></li></ul>

[Hong Kong Customs: Dutiable commodities](https://www.customs.gov.hk/en/service-enforcement-information/trade-facilitation/dutiable-commodities/index.html)

</details>

<details class="tr-more"><summary><i class="ic ic-map-pin" aria-hidden="true"></i>EU in detail</summary>

<ul class="tr-choose"><li><i class="ic ic-calendar-check" aria-hidden="true"></i><b>From 1 July 2026: €3 duty even at €150 or less</b><span>The duty exemption for parcels of €150 or less from outside the EU ended on 30 June 2026. Instead, €3 is charged per type of item (tariff classification). This runs until 1 July 2028; after that, normal duty rates by type of goods apply ㉑.</span></li><li><i class="ic ic-list-checks" aria-hidden="true"></i><b>Counted by type, not quantity</b><span>The EU's examples: 5 T-shirts = €3 (1 type); 1 T-shirt + 1 watch = €6 (2 types) ㉑.</span></li><li><i class="ic ic-hand-coins" aria-hidden="true"></i><b>Who pays</b><span>The declarant, that is the seller or importer. The consumer pays only "in very residual cases" ㉑.</span></li><li><i class="ic ic-info" aria-hidden="true"></i><b>Handling fee</b><span>The EU said it would add a handling fee. In the June 2026 announcement, the amount and start date were "to be determined" in autumn 2026 ㉑.</span></li></ul>

[European Commission: the €3 flat customs duty](https://taxation-customs.ec.europa.eu/news/guidance-and-legal-text-temporary-flat-fee-low-value-imports-which-will-apply-until-1-july-2028-2026-06-08_en)

</details>

<details class="tr-more"><summary><i class="ic ic-map-pin" aria-hidden="true"></i>Japan in detail</summary>

<ul class="tr-choose"><li><i class="ic ic-percent" aria-hidden="true"></i><b>Personal use is valued at 60% of what you paid</b><span>For goods you bought abroad for your own use, the taxable value is the price you actually paid × 0.6 ⑥. For international post, postage and insurance are included in the taxable value ③.</span></li><li><i class="ic ic-circle-check" aria-hidden="true"></i><b>Taxable value ¥10,000 or less: no duty or consumption tax</b><span>For post, this is per box. Parcels split because of weight are added together ⑤.</span></li><li><i class="ic ic-triangle-alert" aria-hidden="true"></i><b>Careful with clothes: duty even under ¥10,000</b><span>Knitted clothing (T-shirts, sweaters and so on), leather bags, handbags and gloves, and leather shoes are not exempt even under ¥10,000 ⑤.</span></li><li><i class="ic ic-droplets" aria-hidden="true"></i><b>Cosmetics duty: none (duty-free)</b><span>Toner, lipstick, perfume and other cosmetics carry no customs duty (Japan Customs' rough guide, as of 1 April 2026; it can differ by origin or material) ⑦. Consumption tax is separate, standard 10% ⑧.</span></li><li><i class="ic ic-package" aria-hidden="true"></i><b>How many cosmetics: up to 24 of the same product</b><span>For personal use, up to 24 per product in standard sizes can come in without a separate import confirmation ⑨.</span></li><li><i class="ic ic-mail" aria-hidden="true"></i><b>How you pay</b><span>By international post with tax of ¥10,000 or less, you pay the tax on delivery. Japan Post adds a handling fee (customs clearance fee) ③. With international express couriers, the company usually handles customs for you and may charge a fee ④.</span></li></ul>

[Japan Customs: personal import procedures (in Japanese)](https://www.customs.go.jp/tsukan/kojinyunyu.htm)

</details>

<details class="tr-more"><summary><i class="ic ic-map-pin" aria-hidden="true"></i>Taiwan in detail</summary>

<ul class="tr-choose"><li><i class="ic ic-circle-check" aria-hidden="true"></i><b>Courier NT$2,000 or less: exempt</b><span>If one courier shipment has a taxable value of NT$2,000 or less, you don't pay customs duty or the business tax and commodity tax customs collects ⑳.</span></li><li><i class="ic ic-ban" aria-hidden="true"></i><b>When the exemption doesn't apply</b><span>Tobacco and alcohol, agricultural products under tariff quotas, and frequent importers: more than 6 exempt shipments in half a year (January–June, July–December) ⑳.</span></li><li><i class="ic ic-truck" aria-hidden="true"></i><b>How you pay</b><span>The courier pays the tax first and collects it from you ⑳.</span></li></ul>

[Taiwan Customs: courier shipments FAQ (in Chinese)](https://web.customs.gov.tw/singlehtml/1207?cntId=cus1_93437_1207)
