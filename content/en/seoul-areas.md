## Arrival day and the last morning

Picture arrival day first, because that is where the area choice shows. Stay in Myeongdong and limousine bus 6001 takes you from the airport to Myeongdong Station in about 1 hour 20 minutes, onto the street where visitors do most of their shopping. Stay in Hongdae and the AREX all-stop train runs to Hongik Univ. in about an hour, no change.

Our pick for the ending is the last morning by Seoul Station: you check in and drop your bags at the station's city airport terminal, then ride the AREX Express nonstop to the airport in 43 minutes with your suitcase already on its way.

## The short answer

| If you are | Stay in | Because |
|---|---|---|
| On a first trip and want it easy | Myeongdong | Bus 6001 from the airport, and Seoul's main shopping street for visitors |
| On a short trip or catching an early flight | Seoul Station | The AREX Express runs nonstop to the airport, and some airlines let you check in at the station |
| Out late, or here for the street life | Hongdae | The AREX all-stop train goes straight to Hongik Univ. |
| Here for palaces, clothes, cafés or a concert | See "The other four" | Each suits one kind of trip |

Our pick for most readers is the first three. Each has a one-seat ride from Incheon, and that matters more on arrival day than any neighborhood mood.

## The three we'd pick first

### Myeongdong: the default for a first trip

Myeongdong is where visitors shop. Foreign cards spent 27.8% of their national total there in May 2026 (Korea Tourism Organization data, reported by the Korea Herald), and more than 20 large fashion and beauty stores opened in the area in two years, Olive Young among them (see our [Olive Young](/olive-young/) and [tax refund](/tax-refund/) guides).

- **From the airport:** bus 6001, about 1 hr 20 min, or AREX to Seoul Station then Line 4, about 1 hr 10 min.
- **Honest downside:** it is built around visitors.

### Seoul Station: best for short stays and early flights

Seoul Station is a rail hub, not a neighborhood to wander: KTX, Lines 1 and 4, and the AREX Express, nonstop to the airport in about 43 minutes for ₩13,000.

The reason to stay here is the **city airport terminal** on B2. With an AREX Express ticket you can check in, drop your bags and clear passport control at the station (check-in 05:20–19:00), then use a dedicated departure lane at Incheon. Check-in closes 3 hours before departure for Terminal 1 and 3 hours 20 minutes for Terminal 2. It covers same-day international flights from Incheon, and only some airlines take part. Details are in [your last day](/departure-day/).

- **Honest downside:** the wrong exit costs time. Check the exit number in Naver Map before you set off.

### Hongdae: the young, late-night choice

Hongdae is the area around Hongik University, the city's best-known late-night district. On Halloween 2026 the city estimated up to 125,000 people there in one night, about four times the Itaewon crowd.

- **From the airport:** the AREX all-stop train goes straight to Hongik Univ. in about an hour, ₩4,650 by transport card. Bus 6002 takes about 1 hr 10 min.
- **Honest downside:** it draws crowds late into the night (see the Halloween estimate above). Light sleepers should pick a hotel a few blocks from the main streets.

## The other four, and who they suit

### Jongno, Insadong and Euljiro: for old Seoul

Stay here for the palaces, Insadong, and Euljiro's bars in old printing alleys, which locals nickname "Hipjiro". Buses 6002 (Jongno), 6011 (Gyeongbokgung and Changdeokgung) and 6701 (Gwanghwamun, City Hall, Euljiro) come from the airport.

- **Honest downside:** no AREX station, so you take a bus or change trains. Bukchon's red zone is open to visitors only 10:00–17:00 ([palaces guide](/palaces/)). Euljiro is changing fast as rents rise and print shops move out.

### Dongdaemun: for clothes shopping and night owls

The Korea Times calls Dongdaemun the city's "24-hour fashion center", with Dongdaemun Design Plaza at its heart. Buses 6001 and 6702 come from the airport; landing late, night bus N6701 stops at Seoul Station, Euljiro and Dongdaemun Design Plaza, and N6002 at Hongdae and Dongdaemun.

- **Honest downside:** it is busiest at night (the Korea Times calls it a '24-hour fashion hub'). Light sleepers should check where the hotel sits.

### Seongsu: for a second trip

Seongsu is the old shoe-making district, now full of pop-up stores and cafés, on Line 2. Seongdong-gu, which includes Seongsu, saw foreign visitors rise 60.2% year on year, against 25.5% for Jung-gu, where Myeongdong is.

- **From the airport:** limousine bus 6013 stops in Seongsu-dong; by train, AREX to Hongik Univ. and change to Line 2.
- **Honest downside:** no rail link to the airport, queues at popular pop-ups, and a trip from the palaces.

### Gangnam: for concerts, COEX and business

Gangnam is the one area here south of the Han River. KSPO Dome, where many K-pop concerts happen, is in Songpa District on the same side. If your concert is at Gocheok Sky Dome (Guro District), Gangnam is the wrong side of the city.

- **From the airport:** bus 6020, about 1 hr 20 min; by train about 1 hr 50 min with a change. Take the bus.
- **Honest downside:** the farthest of these from the palaces, and the longest airport trip by rail.

## Before you book

1. Write down what your trip is for in one line: shopping, palaces, nights out, a concert.
2. Pick the area from the table, then open Naver Map and check the walk from the nearest station exit to the hotel door.
3. Note your airport bus number or train for arrival day.

## What we left out, and who doesn't need this

We left out Itaewon, Yeouido, Jamsil and Sinchon. They're fine, but this page covers only the seven above. We don't compare hotel prices, because they change by date and we can't check them fairly. If your hotel is already booked, go straight to [Incheon Airport to Seoul](/airport-to-seoul/). Before you land, read up on [money](/money/).
