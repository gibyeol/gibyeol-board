## Quick call

| If this is you | Choose | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Your phone takes an eSIM and is not locked | eSIM | Install it at home before you fly, switch it on when you land. Your own SIM stays in the phone |
| Your phone does not take an eSIM | A local SIM | You only have to swap SIM cards |
| A short trip, and you need calls and texts on your own number | Your carrier's roaming | The simplest setup. Compare the prices in your carrier's app before you leave |

The eSIM is easier for a simple reason: no SIM pin, no tiny card to lose, and the SIM you use now stays in the phone while data comes from the eSIM.

## Check 1: does your phone support eSIM?

**iPhone.** iPhone XS, XS Max, XR and later models support eSIM. iPhones sold in Korea have both an eSIM and a physical SIM slot.

**Galaxy.** In Korea, eSIM activation started on 1 September 2022, and the first Korean-market eSIM models were the Galaxy Z Fold4 and Z Flip4. Korean-market models released before that probably do not support it, so do not guess from the model name. Check directly:

- Galaxy: in **Settings → About phone → Status information**, if you see an **EID** entry, the phone supports eSIM.
- iPhone: XS, XR or later supports it, so you only need the model (**Settings → General → About → Model Name**).

## Check 2: is your phone free of a carrier lock?

To use another company's eSIM, the phone must not be locked to one carrier. Even if the EID shows, a lock can stop an eSIM from abroad working.

- iPhone: in **Settings → General → About → Carrier Lock**, "No SIM restrictions" means you pass.
- Galaxy has no such menu. If you bought the phone from a carrier, the quickest answer is to ask that carrier's customer centre before you fly.

## If your phone passes both checks

Finish the install on home Wi-Fi a day or two before you fly. The most common mistake is hunting for a QR code at the airport while boarding is being called.

1. Pick a data plan for the country and the length of your trip
2. Install the eSIM from the QR code or the app (install only, do not switch it on yet)
3. When you land, switch on the eSIM line and allow data roaming on that line
4. Turn data off on the SIM you normally use, so no roaming data charges build up on it
