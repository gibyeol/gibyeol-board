<h2 id="label">Read a label in 30 seconds: four spots on the back</h2>

Korean law requires the same items on every cosmetics box, printed in Korean (other languages may be added). Learn them once and you can read any box at Olive Young, Daiso or a convenience store.

{{fig:kbeauty-how-to-choose-1|Where to look on the back of the box (illustration, not a real brand)}}

<h3 class="tr-step"><span class="n">1</span><i class="ic ic-shield-check" aria-hidden="true"></i>Find the word 기능성화장품 or its mark</h3>

A brightening (미백), wrinkle-care (주름개선) or sunscreen (자외선 차단) product must carry it. It means the product was reviewed by, or reported to, Korea's Ministry of Food and Drug Safety (MFDS). Next to it is the **효능·효과** (efficacy) line naming the reviewed function: brightening, wrinkle care, UV protection and so on.

<aside class="tr-note"><i class="ic ic-triangle-alert" aria-hidden="true"></i><span>If the mark is missing but the front says "brightening" or "wrinkle" in big letters, that is advertising, not a reviewed function.</span></aside>

<h3 class="tr-step"><span class="n">2</span><i class="ic ic-sun" aria-hidden="true"></i>For sunscreen, read SPF and PA</h3>

SPF is a number (anything 50 or above is printed **SPF50+**); PA is plus signs (PA+ to PA++++). Which one to pick is just below in [Pick a sunscreen](#sun).

<h3 class="tr-step"><span class="n">3</span><i class="ic ic-list-checks" aria-hidden="true"></i>If fragrance bothers you, read the 전성분 line</h3>

Every ingredient follows the word 전성분. Look for 향료 (fragrance) and the [allergen names below](#scent). If fragrance doesn't bother you, skip this step.

<h3 class="tr-step"><span class="n">4</span><i class="ic ic-calendar-check" aria-hidden="true"></i>Check the expiry</h3>

Every box shows either **사용기한** (use by this date) or **개봉 후 사용기간** (months of use after opening) together with the production date. Worth a look when you buy several sale sets at once.

<details class="tr-more"><summary><i class="ic ic-info" aria-hidden="true"></i>What every box must show, and why minis differ</summary>

Under Article 10 of the Cosmetics Act the outer box shows: product name, company name and address, full ingredients, volume, batch number, expiry or period after opening, price, for functional cosmetics the word 기능성화장품 or its mark plus the reviewed efficacy and directions, and precautions.

**Minis of 10 mL/10 g or less, and samples,** may show only the name, company, price, batch number and expiry. The missing ingredients must be checkable through a phone number or website on the pack, or a booklet kept in the shop. If you need the full list, read it on the full-size box.

**To check before you go:** on MFDS's [Drug Safety Nara](https://nedrug.mfds.go.kr) go to 의약품 등 정보 → 의약품 및 화장품 품목정보 → 기능성화장품제품정보 (심사 or 보고) and search the product (Korean-only site).

</details>

<h2 id="sun">Pick a sunscreen</h2>

The highest number is not automatically the right one. MFDS advises matching it to your day.

<ul class="tr-choose"><li><i class="ic ic-store" aria-hidden="true"></i><b>Indoors, short walks: SPF15 / PA+ or more</b><span>Sightseeing in town, shopping, cafés.</span></li><li><i class="ic ic-sun" aria-hidden="true"></i><b>Long hours in the sun, like sport: SPF50+ / PA+++ or PA++++</b><span>Hiking, the beach, a full day outdoors.</span></li><li><i class="ic ic-droplets" aria-hidden="true"></i><b>Swimming: look for 내수성 or 지속내수성 (water-resistant)</b><span>Still reapply every 1–2 hours.</span></li></ul>

{{fig:kbeauty-how-to-choose-3|SPF is for UVB, PA is for UVA. Match them to your day}}

- SPF is printed as a whole number, and everything from 50 up is **SPF50+**. Above 50 the real difference is small, which is why it is grouped.
- PA runs PA+, PA++, PA+++, PA++++; more plus signs means more UVA protection.
- How to apply: generously, 15 minutes before you go out, and again if you sweat a lot.

A December palace walk and a midsummer day at Haeundae are different purchases. Buy for the days you actually have.

<h2 id="scent">If fragrance bothers you</h2>

Fragrance may be listed as the single word 향료, but 25 fragrance allergens must be named separately above a set level (over 0.01% in rinse-off products, over 0.001% in leave-on products). The names you will see most often:

<ul class="tr-choose"><li><i class="ic ic-list-checks" aria-hidden="true"></i><b>리모넨 limonene · 리날룰 linalool · 시트로넬올 citronellol · 제라니올 geraniol</b><span>유제놀 eugenol · 벤질알코올 benzyl alcohol · 시트랄 citral · 쿠마린 coumarin are on the same list.</span></li></ul>

## Claims to put back on the shelf

Words that sound like medicine or a clinic treatment, such as 세포재생 (cell regeneration), 항염 (anti-inflammatory), 줄기세포 (stem cell), "Botox you apply" or "filler effect", are illegal on cosmetics. In March 2025 MFDS checked 200 online listings and caught 144 false or exaggerated ads of this kind; in August 2024 it found 82 of 100 microneedle listings in breach, including "skin regeneration" and claims of reaching the dermis.

**Rankings and testers are a starting point only.** A ranking shows what sells, not what suits you (we could not find an official explanation of how Olive Young ranks products).

1. Pick two or three products of the kind you want from the ranking.
2. Run the four label checks above on each.
3. If there is a tester, try the texture and scent before buying the real thing.
4. Choose the one that passes the label check and fits your days, even if it ranks lower.

## Taking it home: cabin bag and checked bag

Toner, gel, mist and sunscreen all count as liquids. Decide which bag they go in before you pay.

{{fig:kbeauty-how-to-choose-4|What goes in the cabin bag and the checked bag (Incheon Airport rules)}}

1. **Cabin bag:** 100 mL or less per container, all in one transparent 1 L zip bag per person (Incheon Airport, international flights).
2. **Bigger sizes:** put them in checked baggage. Non-flammable sprays: up to 500 mL per container and 2 L in total.
3. **Liquids bought duty-free:** keep the sealed duty-free liquids bag closed until you board the flight to your final destination. Rules can differ if you transfer at an airport abroad.

Check your airline's rules before you fly too. What goes in which bag on the day you leave: [Departure day](/departure-day/).

## Common mistakes

<ul class="tr-oops"><li><i class="ic ic-triangle-alert" aria-hidden="true"></i><span>Buying on "brightening" or "glow" on the front. Check the back for 기능성화장품.</span></li><li><i class="ic ic-triangle-alert" aria-hidden="true"></i><span>Hunting for the highest SPF for a city trip. Matching your day is enough.</span></li><li><i class="ic ic-triangle-alert" aria-hidden="true"></i><span>Packing a toner over 100 mL in your cabin bag. Security will make you throw it away.</span></li><li><i class="ic ic-triangle-alert" aria-hidden="true"></i><span>Trusting "Botox you apply". That claim is illegal on cosmetics.</span></li></ul>

<details class="tr-more"><summary><i class="ic ic-list-checks" aria-hidden="true"></i>Our call, as a table</summary>

| Situation | Do this | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Buying sunscreen | SPF/PA that fits your day, not the highest number | MFDS says SPF15/PA+ or more is enough indoors and for short time outside |
| Buying brightening or wrinkle care | Only if the box says **기능성화장품** and names the function | That function was reviewed by or reported to MFDS; "glow" on the front was not |
| Sensitive to fragrance | Check 전성분 for 향료 and the allergen names | Above a set level they must be named separately |
| An ad says "cell regeneration", "anti-inflammatory" or "Botox you apply" | Put it back | MFDS says these claims are illegal on cosmetics |

</details>

<details class="tr-more"><summary><i class="ic ic-pencil" aria-hidden="true"></i>Before you go: one line per person, and buying gifts</summary>

The list is the part you can enjoy at home. Write one line for each person you are buying for, yourself included.

1. **What do they want:** sun protection, brightening or wrinkle care, or avoiding fragrance. That tells you which label to check.
2. **What is their day like:** city walking or long hours in strong sun. That is the SPF/PA to look for.
3. **When and where:** the Olive Young sale calendar is on our [Olive Young guide](/olive-young/), cheap small sizes at [Daiso](/daiso/), neighbourhoods in [Myeongdong, Seongsu or Hongdae](/shopping-streets/), current sales on [Deals](/deals/).

{{fig:kbeauty-how-to-choose-2|Choose by what you want: the label to check for each goal}}

**For a gift,** choose for the other person's days and skin, not yours. If you don't know whether fragrance bothers them, check 전성분 for 향료 and the separately named allergens, and pick a size that fits the bag you are taking.

**Not worth buying on hype alone:** products sold like treatments, products promising clinic-like results, and a fourth sunscreen because it just went viral.

</details>
