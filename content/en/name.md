## How it works

- **English and other Latin-alphabet names** come from our checked list of common names, written the way Korean media usually spell them. Names not on the list get an approximate reading, and we say so.
- **Japanese kana** follow the official Korean rules for writing Japanese sounds.
- **Chinese characters and kanji** are read one by one with their Korean sounds, the way Koreans read hanja. 李 becomes 이 (I), 田中 becomes 전중 (Jeon-jung).
- **Hangul** names get the official romanization, syllable by syllable, the way Korean names are written.

It's a toy, not a translation service: a Korean friend may spell your name a little differently, and that's part of the fun. If yours looks wrong, the link under the result goes straight to us.
